# Mission Alignment Review

## Purpose

To review whether ministries, chapters, and affiliated entities remain faithful to theology, mission, governance, finances, and church relationships.

## Adopted Principles

- Mission drift should be identified early.
- Reviews should include doctrine, governance, finance, outcomes, and relationship with local churches.
- Corrective action should be documented.

## Key Decisions

- Annual or periodic review is required for ministries and chapters.
- Major concerns may be escalated to the International Board or Theological Oversight Council.

## International Application

This document is written for an international society. National chapters may localize procedures required by civil law, but they may not alter the Society's theology, mission, reserved powers, or core governance identity without approval under the international governance framework.
