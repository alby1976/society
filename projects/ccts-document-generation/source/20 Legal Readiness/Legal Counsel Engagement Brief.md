# 4. Questions for Legal Counsel

## 4.1 Entity and Governance

- What legal form is suitable for the proposed work?
- Can international reserved powers be included or recognized?
- What local governance requirements apply?
- Are proposed purposes lawful and appropriate?
- What board liability issues should be addressed?

## 4.2 Charity, Fundraising, and Tax

- Can the entity seek charity or nonprofit status?
- What donation receipting or tax rules apply?
- What fundraising rules apply?
- What restricted gift rules apply?
- What reporting or filing obligations apply?

## 4.3 Operations

- What employment, contractor, or volunteer rules apply?
- What privacy and data protection rules apply?
- What safeguarding or mandatory reporting duties apply?
- What insurance should be considered?
- What records must be retained?

## 4.4 Ministry-Specific Questions

- What legal concerns apply to housing?
- What legal concerns apply to grants or student support?
- What legal concerns apply to worker cooperative activity?
- What legal concerns apply to chaplaincy or spiritual care?
- What legal concerns apply to public communications?

# 6. Risk Questions

Ask counsel to identify:

- legal risks;
- required changes;
- prohibited actions;
- recommended sequence;
- documents needing revision;
- filings required;
- approvals needed;
- matters requiring specialist counsel;
- issues requiring board decision.

# 7. Requested Output

Request counsel to provide:

- written summary;
- key legal issues;
- readiness rating;
- recommended next steps;
- document edits if needed;
- list of legal requirements;
- matters requiring further review.
