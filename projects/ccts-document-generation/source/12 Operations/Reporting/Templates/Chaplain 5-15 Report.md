# Chaplain 5-15 Report

## Template Instructions

This template is for Society chaplains.

Chaplain reports require special confidentiality care. The report should communicate ministry activity, general spiritual care themes, referrals, risks, support needed, and prayer themes without unnecessarily disclosing sensitive pastoral details.

Do not include confessional, intimate, medical, family, legal, or highly personal details unless there is a proper reason and the report is marked with the correct confidentiality level.

The Society chaplain serves the Society and supports connection to faithful local churches. The chaplain does not replace pastors, elders, or local church oversight.
