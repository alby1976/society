# Church Advisory Fellowship

## Executive Summary

The Church Advisory Fellowship is an advisory fellowship of faithful Reformed churches that provides counsel, accountability, cooperation, and theological support to the Society.

## Adopted Principles

- The Fellowship advises but does not govern the Society.
- The Fellowship supports healthy relationships with local churches.
- The Fellowship helps preserve the Society's church-supporting identity.
- The Fellowship may help recognize acceptable seminaries for chaplaincy qualifications.

## Key Decisions

- The Fellowship shall not exercise authority over local churches.
- Participation should represent faithful Reformed churches.
- The Fellowship should help the Society avoid becoming a parallel church structure.
