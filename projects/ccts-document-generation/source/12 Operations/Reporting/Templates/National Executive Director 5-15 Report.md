# National Executive Director 5-15 Report

## Template Instructions

This template is for a National Executive Director or equivalent senior leader responsible for a recognized national chapter of the Christian Community Transformation Society.

This report should help the International Society and National Board understand chapter health, ministry progress, legal compliance, finances, risks, staffing, church relationships, mission alignment, and decisions needed.

Use appropriate confidentiality. Do not include unnecessary personal, legal, pastoral, employment, housing, or grant details.
