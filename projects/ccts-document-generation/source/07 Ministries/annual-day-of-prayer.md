# Annual Day of Prayer

## Executive Summary

The Annual Day of Prayer is a Society-wide practice of dependence on God, thanksgiving, confession, intercession, and recommitment to mission.

## Adopted Principles

- Prayer is not decoration; it is part of governance and mission preservation.
- The chaplain may facilitate the Annual Day of Prayer.
- The chaplain may provide a devotional meditation.
- The event should support rather than replace the worship and authority of local churches.

## Suggested Elements

- Scripture reading.
- Confession and thanksgiving.
- Prayer for churches.
- Prayer for Society ministries.
- Prayer for workers, tenants, students, leaders, and families served.
- Devotional meditation.
- Review of mission and dependence on God.
