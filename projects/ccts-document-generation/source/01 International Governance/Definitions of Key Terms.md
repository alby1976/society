# Definitions of Key Terms

## Purpose

This document establishes the shared vocabulary of the Christian Community Transformation Society. It is intended to prevent confusion, reduce ambiguity, support international expansion, and preserve theological and governance consistency across all chapters, ministries, boards, committees, workers, volunteers, and partner relationships.

This document should be read together with [[Mission Statement]], [[Vision Statement]], [[Core Values]], [[International Governance Framework]], and [[Document Registry]].

## Governing Principle

Terms used in Society documents should be interpreted according to this document unless a specific policy gives a narrower definition for a specific legal, operational, or pastoral purpose.

Where local law requires a different legal term, the local document should state the legal equivalent while preserving the Society's theological and governance meaning.

---

# 1. Theological Terms

## Scripture

The written Word of God, consisting of the Old and New Testaments, received as the supreme and final authority for faith and life.

## Confessional Standards

The historic Reformed confessions adopted or recognized by the Society for doctrinal identity, accountability, and teaching consistency. These may include the Westminster Standards, the Three Forms of Unity, and other recognized Reformed confessional documents as adopted by the Society.

## Reformed

A theological identity rooted in the authority of Scripture, the sovereignty of God, salvation by grace alone through faith alone in Christ alone, covenant theology, the ordinary means of grace, and confessional accountability.

## Local Church

A visible congregation of Christ's Church governed by its own lawful church officers. The Society supports local churches but does not replace them.

## Lord's Day Worship

The gathered worship of the local church on the Lord's Day. The Society's purpose is not to replace Lord's Day worship but to encourage worship to transform the rest of the week.

## Mercy Ministry

Christian service that responds to human need through compassion, practical help, and Gospel-shaped care while remaining accountable to biblical truth.

## Discipleship

The process by which believers are taught, formed, encouraged, and equipped to follow Jesus Christ in all areas of life.

---

# 2. Society and International Structure

## Society

The Christian Community Transformation Society as an organized Christian body established to advance its mission through governance, ministries, affiliated organizations, national chapters, and supporting institutions.

## International Society

The worldwide expression of the Society, consisting of the international governing structure, shared theological identity, international policies, national chapters, and recognized ministries.

## National Chapter

A country-level expression of the Society incorporated or recognized according to local law and authorized to operate under the Society's international constitution, doctrine, mission, governance standards, and reserved powers.

## Founding Chapter

The national chapter first established to organize the Society. Canada may serve as the founding chapter without becoming the theological or governance center of the international Society.

## Regional Ministry

A ministry operating below the national level within a defined province, territory, region, city, or local area.

## Local Ministry

A ministry operated at the community level to serve specific people, churches, workers, families, housing residents, students, or other beneficiaries.

## Affiliated Organization

Any corporation, cooperative, foundation, trust, ministry body, or legal entity created or controlled to support the Society's mission and subject to Society oversight.

## International Expansion

The process by which the Society recognizes, forms, or supports ministry in a new country while preserving theological, governance, and mission continuity.

# 3. Governance Terms

## Governance

The system by which authority, accountability, decisions, policies, stewardship, and mission protection are ordered within the Society.

## International Constitution

The highest internal governing document of the International Society, subject to applicable law and subordinate to Scripture as the Society's supreme theological authority.

## Bylaws

Legal and governance rules adopted by the Society or a national chapter to comply with civil law and implement the Society's constitution and policies.

## Policy

A governing rule approved by the appropriate authority to guide decisions, conduct, operations, and accountability.

## Framework

A structured governance document that establishes principles, relationships, and operating logic without necessarily prescribing every detailed procedure.

## Procedure

A step-by-step process for implementing a policy or framework.

## Charter

A document that establishes the purpose, authority, membership, limits, and responsibilities of a board, council, committee, fellowship, or advisory body.

## Manual

A collected set of policies, procedures, frameworks, templates, and guidance for a major area of Society life.

## Standard

A required benchmark or minimum expectation.

## Guideline

Recommended practice that informs wise action but does not carry the same authority as policy.

## Decision

An approved governance action recorded in the [[Decision Register]].

## Resolution

A formal decision of a board or authorized governing body.

## Reserved Powers

Authorities retained by the International Society and not delegated to national chapters or affiliated organizations. Reserved powers commonly include doctrine, mission, international identity, recognition of chapters, major constitutional changes, and approval of significant affiliated entities.

## Delegated Authority

Authority granted by the International Society or a board to a chapter, officer, committee, staff person, ministry leader, or affiliated organization.

## Authority Level

The level of approval required for a document or decision, such as International Board, National Board, Executive Director, Ministry Leader, or Advisory.

## Mission Drift

A gradual or sudden movement away from the Society's adopted theology, mission, purpose, values, governance standards, or church-supporting identity.

## Mission Alignment Review

A formal review process used to evaluate whether a ministry, chapter, program, partnership, grant, housing initiative, cooperative, or foundation remains aligned with the Society's mission, theology, and governance standards.

---

# 4. Board and Leadership Terms

## International Board

The governing board responsible for the international Society's mission, doctrine protection, constitutional integrity, strategic oversight, reserved powers, international expansion, and stewardship.

## National Board

A board governing a national chapter under local law and under the international governance framework.

## Executive Director

The senior executive officer responsible for day-to-day implementation of the Society's mission under board authority.

## International Executive Director

The senior executive officer responsible for international coordination, chapter support, global strategy, and implementation of international board direction.

## National Executive Director

The senior executive officer responsible for a national chapter's operations, ministries, staff, legal compliance, and reporting.

## Stewardship Advisors

Former board members, founders, or experienced leaders appointed to preserve institutional memory, advise current leadership, and provide historical continuity without exercising governing authority.

## Founder Advisory Role

A non-governing advisory role recognizing the value of founding vision while preventing unhealthy personal control or founder dependency.

## Committee

A smaller body appointed to carry out defined work under delegated authority.

## Council

A body established to provide specialized counsel, review, or oversight within defined limits.

---

# 5. Theological Oversight and Church Relationships

## Theological Oversight Council

A council responsible for reviewing theological consistency, doctrinal fidelity, mission drift, ministry concerns, and significant doctrinal questions. It advises and may carry authority only as defined by the Society's governing documents.

## Church Advisory Fellowship

A fellowship of churches, ministers, elders, theologians, or church representatives invited to provide counsel, accountability, cooperation, and wisdom. It is advisory unless specific authority is granted by governing documents.

## Church Partner

A local church or church body that cooperates with the Society while retaining its own ecclesiastical authority.

## Seminary Recognition

A process by which the Church Advisory Fellowship or designated theological authority recognizes seminaries whose theological training may satisfy Society standards for chaplaincy or leadership roles.

## Ecclesiastical Authority

The authority Christ gives to the church through its lawful officers. The Society does not exercise church discipline, administer sacraments, or replace pastoral oversight.

---

# 6. Membership Terms

## Member

A person admitted into membership according to the Society's membership policy and covenant.

## Voting Member

A member with voting rights as defined by the bylaws.

## Supporting Member

A member or supporter who endorses the mission and contributes prayer, service, finances, or encouragement but may not possess voting rights.

## Founding Member

A member involved in the Society's establishment, subject to any special recognition or transition rules adopted by the Society.

## Honorary Member

A person recognized for significant service, counsel, or support without necessarily holding voting rights.

## Membership Covenant

A document describing the commitments, expectations, mission alignment, and conduct required of members.

## Member Discipline

The Society's process for correcting, suspending, or removing members for serious misconduct, mission misalignment, or violation of governing standards. This is organizational discipline, not church discipline.

---

# 7. Ministry Terms

## Ministry

Any Society activity, program, service, project, or institution intended to advance the Society's mission.

## Ministry Leader

A person responsible for managing or coordinating a ministry under delegated authority.

## Volunteer

A person serving without employment compensation under Society supervision.

## Staff Member

An employee or contracted worker serving the Society or an affiliated organization.

## Beneficiary

A person, family, church, ministry, worker, student, resident, or community receiving assistance or service from the Society.

## Capacity Building

Strengthening the long-term ability of a person, church, ministry, organization, or community to fulfill its calling and responsibilities.

---

# 8. Chaplaincy Terms

## Chaplain

A person appointed by the Society to provide spiritual care, prayer, encouragement, pastoral support within defined limits, and connection to local churches. A Society chaplain does not replace the local church, its elders, or ordained pastoral ministry.

## Minister

A minister of the Word or pastor lawfully recognized by a church body. In Reformed circles, this ordinarily includes ecclesiastical calling, ordination, and accountability to church courts or assemblies.

## Spiritual Care

Biblical encouragement, prayer, listening, counsel, visitation, crisis support, and referral to church leadership where appropriate.

## Annual Day of Prayer

A Society-wide day set aside for thanksgiving, confession, supplication, intercession, mission renewal, and prayer for churches, workers, beneficiaries, chapters, and ministries.

---

# 9. Housing Terms

## Housing Trust

A ministry structure designed to steward land, housing, leases, affordability, and community life under the Society's mission and governance oversight.

## Community Land Trust

A structure in which land is stewarded for long-term community benefit, affordability, and mission alignment.

## Affordable Housing

Housing offered below or within a defined affordability standard for the purpose of serving households, workers, families, or communities in need.

## Long-Term Lease

A lease arrangement that may allow long occupancy or stewardship use while preserving ownership or mission control in the trust or Society.

## Stewardship Housing

Housing understood not merely as property management but as a ministry of care, stability, discipleship, hospitality, and community support.

## Resident

A person or household occupying Society-related housing under a lease, agreement, or ministry arrangement.

---

# 10. Worker Cooperative Terms

## Worker Cooperative

An enterprise owned, governed, or operated for the benefit of workers according to cooperative principles and under Society mission oversight where affiliated with the Society.

## Cooperative Member

A worker admitted to membership in the cooperative according to its rules.

## Cooperative Board

The body responsible for governing the cooperative within its legal structure and any Society oversight agreement.

## Patronage

A cooperative mechanism for allocating surplus or benefits according to member participation, subject to law, cooperative bylaws, and Society mission controls.

## Ownership Transition

The process by which an existing company, cooperative, or enterprise may become affiliated with, donated to, controlled by, or held for the Society's mission.

---

# 11. Church Support Foundation Terms

## Church Support Foundation

A foundation or funding ministry established to support churches, ministry workers, students, church plants, training, capacity building, and related mission-aligned activities.

## Grant

A financial contribution given for an approved purpose subject to eligibility, restrictions, reporting, and accountability.

## Capacity-Building Grant

A grant intended to increase long-term ministry capability rather than merely fund short-term activity.

## Ministry Grant

A grant given to support a defined ministry purpose.

## Scholarship

Financial support for education or training, especially for ministry preparation, theological education, leadership development, or service capacity.

## Restricted Gift

A gift legally or morally restricted to a specific use.

## Endowment

A fund intended to be held long-term, with income or a portion of assets used for mission purposes according to policy.

---

# 12. Finance and Stewardship Terms

## Stewardship

Faithful management of resources entrusted by God for His glory and the good of neighbor.

## Budget

An approved financial plan for a defined period.

## Reserve Fund

Funds set aside for stability, emergencies, obligations, or strategic needs.

## Designated Fund

Funds internally designated for a purpose by the Society.

## Restricted Fund

Funds externally restricted by donor terms, law, or grant conditions.

## Financial Controls

Policies and procedures designed to protect assets, prevent misuse, ensure accountability, and support transparent reporting.

---

# 13. Reporting Terms

## Reporting Framework

The Society's structured system for regular communication, accountability, decision support, and institutional memory.

## 5-15 Report

A concise report designed to take approximately fifteen minutes to prepare and approximately five minutes to read. It summarizes accomplishments, priorities, challenges, decisions needed, upcoming work, and prayer requests.

## Weekly Report

A recurring report normally prepared by workers, volunteers, staff, or ministry leaders.

## Monthly Report

A broader summary normally prepared by ministry leaders, departments, national chapters, or executives.

## Quarterly Report

A governance-level summary prepared for boards, councils, or senior leadership.

## Annual Report

A comprehensive report summarizing ministry, finances, governance, risks, mission alignment, and significant developments over a year.

## Report Retention

The period during which reports must be stored before deletion, archival review, or permanent preservation.

## Institutional Memory

The preserved knowledge, decisions, reports, records, history, lessons, and wisdom that help the Society remain faithful across generations.

---

# 14. Risk, Records, and Privacy Terms

## Risk

A potential event, weakness, condition, or pattern that may harm mission, people, finances, reputation, legal compliance, theology, or operations.

## Risk Management

The process of identifying, evaluating, reducing, monitoring, and reporting risks.

## Records Retention

The policy governing how long records are stored and when they are archived or destroyed.

## Confidential Information

Information that must be protected because of privacy, pastoral sensitivity, employment, legal, financial, or governance concerns.

## Privacy

The responsible protection and use of personal information according to law, policy, and Christian care for neighbor.

---

# 15. International Expansion Terms

## Chapter Recognition

The process by which the International Society formally recognizes a national chapter.

## Localization

Adapting documents, procedures, language, and operations to local law and culture while preserving the Society's mission and doctrine.

## Translation

Rendering an official Society document into another language. Translation does not create a separate authority unless formally approved.

## Local Adaptation

A permitted adjustment to procedures or implementation because of local law, culture, or operational context.

## Non-Negotiable Standards

Requirements that may not be altered by national chapters, including doctrinal identity, mission, reserved powers, and major governance protections.

---

# Review and Amendment

This document should be reviewed annually and whenever the Society adopts a major governance document, launches a national chapter, creates a new affiliated organization, or materially changes reporting or governance systems.
