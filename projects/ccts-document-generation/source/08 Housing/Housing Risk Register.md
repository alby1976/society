# Housing Risk Register

## 1. Purpose

This register provides a standard structure for tracking housing risks across Society-related housing ministries.

Housing risk must be named, owned, reviewed, mitigated, and escalated. A risk that is never written down is not gone; it is simply waiting for a worse time to introduce itself.

---

# 2. Risk Categories

Housing risks may include:

- mission drift;
- legal compliance;
- tenancy or lease disputes;
- resident safety;
- safeguarding;
- privacy;
- financial shortfall;
- debt;
- maintenance failure;
- capital reserve insufficiency;
- property damage;
- insurance;
- donor restrictions;
- church relationship confusion;
- chaplaincy boundary concern;
- staff or volunteer capacity;
- contractor or vendor risk;
- neighborhood conflict;
- discrimination complaint;
- emergency preparedness;
- reputational concern.

---

# 3. Risk Rating

## 3.1 Likelihood

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Low | Unlikely |
| Medium | Possible |
| High | Likely |
| Urgent | Already happening or imminent |

## 3.2 Impact

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Low | Minor inconvenience or limited effect |
| Medium | Significant operational or resident effect |
| High | Serious legal, financial, safety, or mission effect |
| Critical | Severe harm, major legal exposure, mission failure, or crisis |

## 3.3 Overall Risk

Overall risk may be rated:

- Low;
- Medium;
- High;
- Critical.

---

# 4. Escalation

Risks must be escalated when:

- overall risk is High or Critical;
- safety is involved;
- safeguarding is involved;
- legal advice is needed;
- board decision is needed;
- financial sustainability is threatened;
- mission alignment is compromised;
- property condition may endanger residents;
- complaints reveal a pattern;
- a serious incident occurs.

The following standard risks should be considered when launching or reviewing housing ministry.

| Risk ID | Risk Description | Category | Default Review |
|---|---|---|---|
| HOUSE-RISK-MISSION-001 | Housing becomes mere property management and loses ministry purpose | Mission Alignment | Annual |
| HOUSE-RISK-LEGAL-001 | Lease or eligibility criteria fail legal review | Legal | Before use |
| HOUSE-RISK-RESIDENT-001 | Resident privacy is mishandled | Privacy | Quarterly |
| HOUSE-RISK-SAFETY-001 | Property safety issue harms resident or visitor | Safety | Monthly |
| HOUSE-RISK-MAINT-001 | Deferred maintenance creates major cost or unsafe condition | Maintenance | Quarterly |
| HOUSE-RISK-FIN-001 | Affordability model becomes financially unsustainable | Finance | Quarterly |
| HOUSE-RISK-CHURCH-001 | Housing ministry confuses Society role with church authority | Church Relationship | Annual |
| HOUSE-RISK-COERCION-001 | Religious participation is perceived as coerced by housing need | Mission / Legal | Annual |

# 7. Risk Review Meeting

Housing risk should be reviewed at least quarterly during active operations.

A risk review meeting should ask:

- What new risks have emerged?
- Which risks increased?
- Which risks decreased?
- Which risks need escalation?
- Which risks need board decision?
- Which risks require policy revision?
- Which risks affect residents?
- Which risks affect mission alignment?
- Which risks require legal or professional advice?

---

# 8. Risk Mitigation

Mitigation may include:

- policy revision;
- training;
- legal review;
- maintenance action;
- reserve funding;
- staffing change;
- vendor replacement;
- resident support plan;
- complaint resolution;
- safety upgrade;
- lease revision;
- mission alignment review;
- board decision;
- pause on growth;
- property disposal review.

---

# 9. Reporting

Risk summaries should be included in:

- [[Housing Manager 5-15 Report]];
- monthly housing summary;
- board reports;
- [[Annual Housing Report]];
- [[Housing Mission Alignment Review]].

Reports should protect resident confidentiality.

---

# 10. Closure

A risk may be closed when:

- the risk no longer exists;
- mitigation is complete;
- responsibility has transferred;
- the relevant project ended;
- board or housing authority approves closure.

Closure should be documented with reasons.

---

# 11. Review

This register should be reviewed quarterly during active housing operations and updated whenever significant incidents occur.
