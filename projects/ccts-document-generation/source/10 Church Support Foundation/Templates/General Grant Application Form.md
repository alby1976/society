# General Grant Application Form

## Template Instructions

This form is used for general Church Support Foundation grant applications.

Use this form for ordinary grants unless a more specific form applies, such as [[Capacity-Building Grant Application Form]] or [[Ministry Student Support Application Form]].

Applicants should answer clearly and briefly. The Foundation may request additional information if the grant is large, complex, restricted, cross-border, theological, or high-risk.
