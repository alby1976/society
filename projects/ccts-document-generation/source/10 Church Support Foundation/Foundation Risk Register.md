# Foundation Risk Register

## 1. Purpose

This register provides a standard structure for tracking risks in the Church Support Foundation.

Foundation risks must be named, owned, reviewed, mitigated, and escalated. In grant-making, unmanaged risk often hides behind good intentions and polite committee minutes.

---

# 2. Risk Categories

Foundation risks may include:

- mission drift;
- donor control;
- unhealthy donor restrictions;
- grant misuse;
- recipient non-reporting;
- theological misalignment;
- church relationship confusion;
- private benefit;
- conflict of interest;
- student support uncertainty;
- weak financial controls;
- restricted fund tracking failure;
- privacy breach;
- legal or charity compliance;
- grant dependency;
- poor documentation;
- cross-border granting;
- reputational concern;
- governance capacity;
- emergency grant misuse;
- insider influence.

---

# 3. Risk Rating

## 3.1 Likelihood

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Low | Unlikely |
| Medium | Possible |
| High | Likely |
| Urgent | Already happening or imminent |

## 3.2 Impact

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Low | Minor inconvenience or limited effect |
| Medium | Significant grant, recipient, donor, or governance effect |
| High | Serious financial, legal, doctrinal, church, or mission effect |
| Critical | Severe harm, major legal exposure, major misuse, mission failure, or crisis |

## 3.3 Overall Risk

Overall risk may be rated:

- Low;
- Medium;
- High;
- Critical.

---

# 4. Escalation

Risks must be escalated when:

- overall risk is High or Critical;
- legal or charity compliance is involved;
- donor restriction may compromise mission;
- grant misuse is suspected;
- private benefit concern arises;
- doctrinal concern arises;
- church relationship concern arises;
- conflict of interest affects a grant decision;
- restricted funds cannot be used as intended;
- recipient reporting failure is repeated;
- board decision is needed.

# 6. Standard Foundation Risks

The following standard risks should be considered when launching or reviewing Foundation ministry.

| Risk ID | Risk Description | Category | Default Review |
|---|---|---|---|
| FOUND-RISK-MISSION-001 | Foundation drifts into general philanthropy without clear church-supporting mission | Mission Alignment | Annual |
| FOUND-RISK-DONOR-001 | Donor restrictions distort mission priorities or grant decisions | Donor Restrictions | Quarterly |
| FOUND-RISK-GRANT-001 | Grant funds are used outside approved purpose | Grant Stewardship | Quarterly |
| FOUND-RISK-REPORT-001 | Recipients fail to report and future grants continue anyway | Accountability | Quarterly |
| FOUND-RISK-PRIVATE-001 | Grant creates improper private benefit or insider advantage | Legal / Governance | Before approval |
| FOUND-RISK-CHURCH-001 | Funding creates improper influence over church governance | Church Relationship | Before approval |
| FOUND-RISK-THEO-001 | Grant supports doctrinally misaligned teaching or training | Theology | Before approval |
| FOUND-RISK-STUDENT-001 | Student support lacks clear terms for program change or withdrawal | Student Support | Before approval |
| FOUND-RISK-RESTRICTED-001 | Restricted funds are not tracked or used correctly | Finance | Monthly |
| FOUND-RISK-CONFLICT-001 | Conflicts of interest affect grant review | Governance | Every application |
| FOUND-RISK-PRIVACY-001 | Sensitive applicant, donor, student, or church information is mishandled | Privacy | Quarterly |

---

# 7. Risk Review Meeting

Foundation risk should be reviewed at least quarterly during active operations.

A risk review meeting should ask:

- What new risks have emerged?
- Which risks increased?
- Which risks decreased?
- Which risks need escalation?
- Which risks need board decision?
- Which risks require policy revision?
- Which risks affect recipients?
- Which risks affect donor relationships?
- Which risks affect mission alignment?
- Which risks require legal, theological, or church advisory review?

---

# 8. Risk Mitigation

Mitigation may include:

- grant suspension;
- revised grant agreement;
- donor restriction refusal;
- legal review;
- theological review;
- church advisory review;
- conflict management;
- recipient follow-up;
- repayment request where lawful;
- financial controls improvement;
- reporting simplification;
- restricted fund review;
- privacy training;
- board decision;
- mission alignment review.

---

# 9. Reporting

Risk summaries should be included in:

- [[Foundation Director 5-15 Report]];
- grant committee reports;
- board reports;
- [[Annual Foundation Report]];
- [[Foundation Mission Alignment Review]].

Reports should protect applicant, donor, recipient, student, and church confidentiality.

---

# 10. Closure

A risk may be closed when:

- the risk no longer exists;
- mitigation is complete;
- responsibility has transferred;
- the relevant grant or fund closed;
- board or Foundation authority approves closure.

Closure should be documented with reasons.

---

# 11. Review

This register should be reviewed quarterly during active Foundation operations and updated whenever significant grant, donor, or compliance concerns arise.
