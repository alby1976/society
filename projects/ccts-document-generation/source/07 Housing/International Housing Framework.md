# International Housing Framework

## Purpose

To define housing ministry as an international ministry framework implemented locally according to law.

## Adopted Principles

- Housing can support Christian community and mercy without coercion.
- The Society should prefer long-term stewardship structures such as a land trust or equivalent local model.
- Housing ministry must not require false profession or replace church life.
- Local law controls lease mechanics; international mission controls purpose.

## Key Decisions

- Housing ministries may be country-specific entities under Society oversight.
- Non-Christian families may be served.
- Christian community expectations should be clear, charitable, lawful, and non-coercive.

## International Application

This document is written for an international society. National chapters may localize procedures required by civil law, but they may not alter the Society's theology, mission, reserved powers, or core governance identity without approval under the international governance framework.
