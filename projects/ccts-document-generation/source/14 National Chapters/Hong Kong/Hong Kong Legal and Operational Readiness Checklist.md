# Hong Kong Legal and Operational Readiness Checklist

## 1. Purpose

This checklist helps the Hong Kong formation team identify legal, governance, operational, financial, church relationship, and ministry readiness items before any legal formation, fundraising, public launch, hiring, grant-making, housing, or cooperative activity.

This document is not legal advice. Qualified Hong Kong legal, accounting, employment, privacy, fundraising, housing, and organizational advice should be obtained before action.

---

# 2. Governing Principle

The Society should not enter a jurisdiction with more confidence than knowledge.

Legal and operational readiness protects mission, people, churches, donors, leaders, and public witness.
