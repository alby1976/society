# International Church Advisory Fellowship

## Purpose

To provide international church counsel, theological wisdom, seminary recognition support, and relational accountability.

## Adopted Principles

- The Fellowship advises but does not govern the Society.
- It should include faithful Reformed church representation across countries as the Society grows.
- It helps preserve church-strengthening posture.

## Key Decisions

- The Fellowship may advise on chaplain qualifications and seminary recognition.
- It should not become a replacement for local church courts or denominational governance.

## International Application

This document is written for an international society. National chapters may localize procedures required by civil law, but they may not alter the Society's theology, mission, reserved powers, or core governance identity without approval under the international governance framework.
