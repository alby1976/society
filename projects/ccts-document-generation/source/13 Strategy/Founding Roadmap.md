# Founding Roadmap

## Purpose

To define the staged founding path for the Society as an international organization founded initially through the Canadian chapter.

## Adopted Principles

- Prayer accompanies every major stage.
- Leadership development comes before complex ministry expansion.
- Pilot cooperative comes before the foundation because the foundation requires more resources.
- International architecture is established before chapters multiply.

## Key Decisions

- Roadmap order: prayer, vision, theology, international governance, Canadian founding chapter, leadership development, pilot cooperative, church relationships, chaplaincy, housing, foundation, international expansion readiness.

## International Application

This document is written for an international society. National chapters may localize procedures required by civil law, but they may not alter the Society's theology, mission, reserved powers, or core governance identity without approval under the international governance framework.
