# Mission Theology

## Purpose

To explain why the Society exists: not to replace Lord’s Day worship, but to help Lord’s Day worship transform the rest of the week.

## Adopted Principles

- Worship is central.
- Mercy, work, housing, leadership, and community transformation flow from the Gospel.
- The Society strengthens local churches by helping Christians live faithfully throughout the week.

## Key Decisions

- The Society is not a church.
- Ministry activity must point people toward faithful local churches.
- Chaplaincy and mercy ministry must not contradict church leadership.

## International Application

This document is written for an international society. National chapters may localize procedures required by civil law, but they may not alter the Society's theology, mission, reserved powers, or core governance identity without approval under the international governance framework.
