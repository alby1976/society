# Board Governance Framework

## Executive Summary

The Board governs the Society by stewarding mission, doctrine, finances, strategy, risk, and executive accountability. The Board does not replace theological, church, or advisory functions, but remains the Society's governing body.

## Adopted Principles

- Governance protects mission.
- Board authority must be clear, accountable, and bounded.
- Board decisions should be prayerful and documented.
- The Board should preserve theological identity across leadership transitions.

## Board Responsibilities

- Steward mission, purpose, and vision.
- Approve policies and strategic plans.
- Oversee finances and risk.
- Supervise executive leadership.
- Maintain governance documents.
- Ensure mission alignment review.
- Receive theological and church advisory input.

## Key Decisions

- Mission drift prevention shall be built into governance.
- The Board governs; advisory bodies advise.
