# Foundation Director 5-15 Report

## Template Instructions

This template is for directors or leaders responsible for a Church Support Foundation or grant-making ministry connected to the Society.

The report should help leaders understand grant activity, funding, donor restrictions, church relationships, scholarship matters, capacity-building work, financial stewardship, risks, and decisions needed.

Do not include unnecessary private financial, student, family, church, donor, or grant applicant details. Use the correct confidentiality level.
