# Housing Trust

## Executive Summary

The Housing Trust is envisioned as a Christian housing ministry that supports affordability, community, discipleship, and church-connected life while remaining legally prudent and non-coercive.

## Adopted Principles

- Housing ministry may advance religion where it supports Christian community, mercy, discipleship, and church participation.
- The Society should prefer a community land trust or housing corporation structure that remains under doctrinal and governance oversight.
- Long-term lease models may support affordability and stewardship.
- Christian community expectations may be encouraged without coercive or improper requirements.

## Key Decisions

- Community Land Trust is preferred over an ordinary housing corporation where it better preserves mission and land stewardship.
- The Society may rent or lease to non-Christian families.
- Church attendance and devotional participation should be handled carefully to avoid coercion or legal problems.
- Housing should not replace the church.
