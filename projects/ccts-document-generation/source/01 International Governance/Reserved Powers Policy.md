# Reserved Powers Policy

## Purpose

To identify powers that remain with the international Society and may not be independently exercised by national chapters.

## Adopted Principles

- Theology, mission, chapter recognition, dissolution, name use, and international standards are reserved powers.
- Delegated authority is real but limited.
- Reserved powers protect unity without micromanaging local operations.

## Key Decisions

- National chapters may not alter the Statement of Faith.
- National chapters may not form independent ministries using the Society name without recognition.
- International approval is required for constitutional change, chapter suspension, and major theological deviation.

## International Application

This document is written for an international society. National chapters may localize procedures required by civil law, but they may not alter the Society's theology, mission, reserved powers, or core governance identity without approval under the international governance framework.
