# Grant Report Form

## Template Instructions

This form is used for interim or final grant reporting.

The report should be proportionate to the grant. Small grants may require brief answers. Larger, restricted, high-risk, or multi-year grants may require more detail and attachments.

Do not include unnecessary confidential information about students, families, church members, donors, recipients, or vulnerable persons.
