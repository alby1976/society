# Website and Digital Presence Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs the Society's websites, public digital pages, online profiles, public documents, and digital ministry presence.

The website and digital presence should accurately reflect the Society's mission, status, doctrine, governance, ministries, and public identity.

---

# 2. Governing Principle

The website is not only a brochure. It is a public governance signal.

A website can create expectations, donor reliance, legal risk, church concerns, and public identity. It must be maintained carefully.

---

# 3. Scope

This policy applies to:

- official website;
- national chapter websites;
- landing pages;
- donation pages;
- social media profiles;
- public document repositories;
- public dashboards;
- public newsletters;
- public forms;
- ministry pages;
- online event pages.

---

# 4. Required Website Clarity

The website should clearly state:

- Society name;
- mission;
- vision;
- church-supporting identity;
- what the Society is not;
- chapter status;
- ministry status;
- governance contact;
- donation status where applicable;
- privacy statement;
- public reports where appropriate.

---

# 5. Website Authority

Website content should have an owner.

Website authority should define:

- who may edit;
- who may approve content;
- who reviews theology;
- who reviews legal or donation language;
- who manages technical access;
- who archives public changes;
- who responds to form submissions.

---

# 6. Chapter Pages

National Chapter pages must accurately state chapter status.

A page should not imply recognition, legal status, charitable status, donation authority, or ministry launch before approval exists.

Formation teams should use approved status language.

---

# 7. Donation Pages

Donation pages require special review.

They should not be published unless:

- legal authority to receive donations is clear;
- tax receipt language is accurate;
- restricted fund language is approved;
- privacy handling is defined;
- payment processor authority is approved;
- financial reporting responsibility is assigned.

---

# 8. Forms

Public forms should collect only necessary information.

Forms may include:

- volunteer interest;
- contact request;
- event registration;
- grant inquiry;
- housing inquiry;
- donor inquiry;
- church contact.

Forms should include privacy notice where needed and should not collect sensitive information unless systems are ready.

---

# 9. Stories and Photos

Stories, testimonies, photos, videos, and case studies require consent and review.

The Society should consider:

- power imbalance;
- vulnerability;
- future risk;
- dignity;
- privacy;
- whether identifying details are necessary;
- whether the person can withdraw consent where applicable.

Do not use vulnerable persons as marketing material.

---

# 10. Public Documents

Public documents should be approved before posting.

Public documents may include:

- mission statement;
- public annual report;
- statement of faith;
- policies suitable for public release;
- financial summaries;
- program descriptions.

Internal, confidential, board confidential, or legal privileged documents should not be posted publicly.

---

# 11. Digital Security

Website and digital presence should follow basic security practices.

This includes:

- limited admin access;
- strong authentication;
- access removal when roles end;
- plugin and software updates;
- backups;
- domain ownership records;
- password management;
- incident response;
- privacy review.

---

# 12. Content Review

Website content should be reviewed regularly.

Review should check:

- accuracy;
- broken links;
- outdated chapter status;
- ministry status;
- donation language;
- privacy statement;
- contact information;
- public documents;
- brand consistency;
- accessibility.

---

# 13. Archiving

Major website changes should be archived where appropriate.

Archive records may include:

- date;
- page changed;
- old content;
- new content;
- approving authority;
- reason for change.

---

# 14. Review

This policy should be reviewed annually and before any major website launch, donation page launch, national chapter page launch, or public campaign.
