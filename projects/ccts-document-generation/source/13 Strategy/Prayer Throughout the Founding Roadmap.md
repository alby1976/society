# Prayer Throughout the Founding Roadmap

## 1. Purpose

This document explains how prayer should accompany the founding roadmap of the Christian Community Transformation Society.

Prayer is not an optional spiritual decoration added to governance. It is an expression of dependence on God before, during, and after decisions.

---

# 2. Governing Principle

The Society should not move through any major founding stage without prayer.

Planning without prayer becomes self-reliance. Prayer without planning can become pious avoidance. The Society needs both dependence and diligence.

---

# 3. Prayer Before Stage 1

Before theology, mission, and purpose are formalized, pray for:

- humility;
- faithfulness to Scripture;
- clarity of mission;
- protection from pride;
- wisdom to define what the Society must not become;
- courage to support local churches rather than replace them.

---

# 4. Prayer Before Stage 2

Before international governance is formed, pray for:

- faithful governance;
- wise use of authority;
- protection from power struggles;
- clarity about reserved powers;
- protection from mission drift;
- unity among founders.

---

# 5. Prayer Before Stage 3

Before founding leaders and advisors are appointed, pray for:

- qualified leaders;
- humility;
- courage;
- freedom from unhealthy ambition;
- protection for leaders' families;
- willingness to reduce workload when needed;
- wisdom to say no to unqualified leadership.

---

# 6. Prayer Before Stage 4

Before reporting and institutional memory systems are implemented, pray for:

- love of truth;
- honest records;
- protection from secrecy;
- future leaders;
- discipline to report;
- wisdom to remember what matters.

---

# 7. Prayer Before Stage 5

Before church advisory relationships are formed, pray for:

- faithful churches;
- teachable Society leaders;
- wise advisors;
- clear boundaries;
- protection from replacing the church;
- partnership without control.

---

# 8. Prayer Before Stage 6

Before legal and financial readiness work begins, pray for:

- lawful conduct;
- financial honesty;
- wise counsel;
- patience;
- protection from reckless commitments;
- integrity with donors;
- clarity before incorporation or fundraising.

---

# 9. Prayer Before Stage 7

Before a worker cooperative pilot begins, pray for:

- meaningful work;
- worker dignity;
- safety;
- fair compensation;
- wise customers;
- skill development;
- honest reporting;
- protection from exploiting vulnerable workers.

---

# 10. Prayer Before Stage 8

Before chaplaincy implementation, pray for:

- qualified chaplains;
- gentle spiritual care;
- confidentiality;
- church referral wisdom;
- protection from role confusion;
- comfort for those in distress;
- faithful local church connection.

---

# 11. Prayer Before Stage 9

Before national chapter formation, pray for:

- wisdom in each country;
- legal clarity;
- local church relationships;
- qualified leaders;
- cultural humility;
- protection from overexpansion;
- unity under the international mission.

---

# 12. Prayer Before Stage 10

Before Foundation readiness and grant-making, pray for:

- faithful generosity;
- protection from donor control;
- wise grant decisions;
- financial accountability;
- protection from private benefit;
- care for students and churches;
- recipient faithfulness.

---

# 13. Prayer Before Stage 11

Before housing feasibility and trust formation, pray for:

- wisdom;
- resident dignity;
- lawful structure;
- financial sustainability;
- protection from coercion;
- maintenance stewardship;
- property decisions that serve people and mission.

---

# 14. Prayer Before Stage 12

Before annual review and expansion, pray for:

- thanksgiving;
- repentance;
- courage to correct;
- wisdom to pause where needed;
- discernment about expansion;
- protection from pride in activity;
- renewed dependence on God.

---

# 15. ACTS Pattern

The Society may use the ACTS pattern for founding prayer.

## Adoration

Praise God for His holiness, wisdom, mercy, providence, and rule over all things.

## Confession

Confess pride, haste, fear, self-reliance, mission drift, and failure to love neighbor.

## Thanksgiving

Give thanks for churches, advisors, workers, volunteers, donors, leaders, beneficiaries, and providential provision.

## Supplication

Ask for wisdom, protection, courage, provision, qualified leaders, faithful churches, and mission clarity.

---

# 16. Prayer Records

The Society may preserve non-confidential prayer records as part of institutional memory.

Prayer records may include:

- date;
- stage;
- prayer themes;
- Scripture used;
- thanksgiving items;
- concerns;
- decisions prayed over;
- follow-up items.

Sensitive personal prayer details should not be archived broadly.

---

# 17. Annual Day of Prayer

The founding roadmap should connect to [[Annual Day of Prayer]].

The Annual Day of Prayer may include:

- thanksgiving for completed stages;
- confession of failures;
- prayer for current stage;
- prayer for future stages;
- prayer for churches;
- prayer for leaders;
- prayer for vulnerable people served by the Society.
