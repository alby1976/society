# International Worker Cooperative Framework

## Purpose

To define worker cooperatives as mission-serving institutions that support meaningful work, discipleship, and sustainable ministry.

## Adopted Principles

- The cooperative is not the church.
- Work is a context for discipleship, service, and stewardship.
- Cooperative entities remain under Society mission oversight.
- Pilot before large-scale expansion.

## Key Decisions

- A pilot cooperative should come before the Church Support Foundation.
- Existing business assets may eventually be donated or transferred if legally and missionally appropriate.
- National cooperative structures must comply with local law.

## International Application

This document is written for an international society. National chapters may localize procedures required by civil law, but they may not alter the Society's theology, mission, reserved powers, or core governance identity without approval under the international governance framework.
