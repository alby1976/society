# Translation and Localization Policy

## Purpose

To govern translation, cultural adaptation, and local implementation of international documents.

## Adopted Principles

- Translation is permitted and encouraged.
- Translation must preserve meaning.
- Local adaptation may change examples, procedures, or legally required terms.
- Local adaptation may not alter theology, mission, reserved powers, or constitutional identity.

## Key Decisions

- Canonical documents should identify the controlling language.
- Translated documents should preserve the same document ID with a language suffix where appropriate.
- Theological translations should be reviewed.

## International Application

This document is written for an international society. National chapters may localize procedures required by civil law, but they may not alter the Society's theology, mission, reserved powers, or core governance identity without approval under the international governance framework.
