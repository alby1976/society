# Canada Chapter Local Adaptation Register

## 1. Purpose

This register tracks Canada-specific adaptations to international Society governance documents, policies, templates, procedures, and public communications.

The register exists to preserve international alignment while allowing lawful Canadian implementation.

---

# 2. Governing Principle

Local adaptation must be documented, reviewed, and bounded.

If an adaptation is not recorded, future leaders may not know whether it was intentional, temporary, legal, accidental, or mission drift.

# 4. Adaptation Categories

Use one or more categories:

- legal structure;
- charity language;
- governance terminology;
- financial reporting;
- donation handling;
- privacy;
- employment;
- volunteer management;
- safeguarding;
- housing;
- worker cooperative;
- Foundation grants;
- church relationships;
- translation;
- public communications;
- local calendar;
- other.

---

# 5. Required Review Fields

Each adaptation should record:

- international source document;
- Canada-specific change;
- reason for change;
- whether legal review was obtained;
- whether International Board approval is required;
- whether mission alignment is affected;
- effective date;
- review date;
- responsible person.

---

# 6. Adaptations Requiring International Approval

Canada Chapter adaptations require International Board or delegated international approval if they affect:

- doctrine;
- mission;
- reserved powers;
- governance authority;
- chapter recognition;
- public identity;
- church recognition;
- chaplain qualifications;
- grant eligibility;
- housing eligibility;
- cooperative structure;
- financial controls;
- reporting requirements;
- conflict of interest;
- safeguarding;
- privacy at a high-risk level.

---

# 7. Minor Local Adaptations

Minor local adaptations may be recorded without formal international approval if they do not affect core identity, authority, risk, or policy meaning.

Examples:

- Canadian spelling;
- local address;
- Canadian date format;
- province-specific contact information;
- local administrative title;
- local meeting schedule;
- local form labels.

---

# 8. Legal Adaptations

Legal adaptations should include a legal note or reference to legal review.

Legal adaptations may involve:

- incorporation form;
- charity language;
- director requirements;
- employment standards;
- privacy rules;
- housing law;
- cooperative law;
- grant-making restrictions;
- fundraising rules;
- receipting rules.

---

# 9. Translation or Multilingual Adaptations

If Canada Chapter uses translated or multilingual documents, the register should record:

- source document;
- target language;
- translator;
- reviewer;
- approval date;
- version;
- theological review if needed;
- legal review if needed.

Translation should not create a different meaning.

---

# 10. Review Schedule

The register should be reviewed:

- quarterly during formation;
- before provisional recognition;
- before full recognition;
- annually after recognition;
- whenever a major policy changes;
- whenever law or ministry structure changes.

---

# 11. Retired Adaptations

When an adaptation is no longer needed, it should be marked retired rather than deleted.

Retirement record should include:

- reason;
- date;
- approving authority;
- replacement document or policy if any.

Institutional memory should preserve why the adaptation existed.

---

# 12. Review

This register should be reviewed quarterly during Canada Chapter formation and at least annually after recognition.
