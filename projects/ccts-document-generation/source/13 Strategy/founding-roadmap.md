# Founding Roadmap

## Executive Summary

The Founding Roadmap sequences the Society's development so that theology, prayer, leadership, governance, and pilot ministry precede complex institutional expansion.

## Adopted Principles

- Prayer accompanies each major stage.
- Leadership development should precede the worker cooperative.
- A pilot cooperative should precede the Church Support Foundation because the Foundation requires more resources.
- Ministries should be built gradually and reviewed for mission alignment.

## Roadmap

1. Prayer.
2. Vision refinement.
3. Governance development.
4. Leadership development.
5. Prayer.
6. Pilot Worker Cooperative.
7. Prayer.
8. Society incorporation.
9. Prayer.
10. Church partnerships.
11. Prayer.
12. Chaplaincy.
13. Prayer.
14. Housing development.
15. Prayer.
16. Church Support Foundation.
17. Prayer.
18. Long-term expansion.

## Key Decisions

- Prayer is required before Stages 2 through 12 and remains integrated afterward.
- Pilot cooperative comes before Foundation.
- Leadership development comes before cooperative launch.
