# Mission Alignment Review

## Executive Summary

The Mission Alignment Review system protects the Society from mission drift by periodically reviewing every ministry, partnership, and supporting organization against the Society's theology, mission, governance, and church-supporting boundaries.

## Adopted Principles

Every ministry should be reviewed for:

- Theological fidelity.
- Mission alignment.
- Financial stewardship.
- Governance accountability.
- Ministry effectiveness.
- Church relationship health.
- Risk and sustainability.

## Key Decisions

- Mission drift should be addressed early.
- Reviews should be documented and linked to reports.
- Theological concerns may be referred to the Theological Oversight Council.
- Church relationship concerns may be referred to the Church Advisory Fellowship.
