# Board Governance Policy

## Purpose

To define board practices for meetings, decisions, accountability, conflicts, review, committees, and governance discipline.

## Adopted Principles

- Board work should be prayerful, orderly, documented, and mission-aligned.
- Conflicts of interest must be disclosed.
- Major decisions should be recorded in the Decision Register.
- Board authority must not undermine local church authority.

## Key Decisions

- Board decisions should use document IDs and decision IDs.
- Board policies should be reviewed annually.
- Governance decisions affecting theology require theological oversight review.

## International Application

This document is written for an international society. National chapters may localize procedures required by civil law, but they may not alter the Society's theology, mission, reserved powers, or core governance identity without approval under the international governance framework.
