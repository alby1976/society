# Master Decision Register

## 1. Purpose

This register preserves major governance, theology, mission, roadmap, chapter, ministry, and operational decisions made during the founding of the Society.

A decision that is not recorded must be rediscovered, relitigated, or guessed by future leaders.

---

# 2. Decision Register Rules

1. Record decisions that affect doctrine, mission, authority, structure, ministry, finance, people, risk, chapters, or public identity.
2. Record the reason, not only the outcome.
3. Link related documents.
4. Record whether a decision is adopted, proposed, deferred, superseded, or rejected.
5. Preserve superseded decisions instead of deleting them.
6. Use stable decision IDs.

---

# 3. Decision Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Adopted | Decision accepted as governing direction |
| Proposed | Draft direction awaiting approval |
| Deferred | Not rejected, but not active |
| Superseded | Replaced by later decision |
| Rejected | Considered and declined |
| Needs Review | Requires future confirmation |
