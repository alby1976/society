# Cooperative Risk Register

## 1. Purpose

This register provides a standard structure for tracking risks in Society-affiliated worker cooperatives.

Cooperative risks must be named, owned, reviewed, mitigated, and escalated. A risk hidden in someone's head is not governed; it is merely waiting for a worse moment.

---

# 2. Risk Categories

Cooperative risks may include:

- mission drift;
- worker exploitation;
- employment misclassification;
- compensation failure;
- payroll or tax failure;
- worker safety;
- customer contract risk;
- customer non-payment;
- data privacy;
- financial loss;
- cash flow;
- insurance gap;
- conflict of interest;
- related-party transactions;
- governance failure;
- weak reporting;
- spiritual coercion;
- church boundary confusion;
- leadership capacity;
- worker conflict;
- customer complaint;
- quality failure;
- legal compliance;
- reputational risk.

---

# 3. Risk Rating

## 3.1 Likelihood

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Low | Unlikely |
| Medium | Possible |
| High | Likely |
| Urgent | Already happening or imminent |

## 3.2 Impact

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Low | Minor inconvenience or limited effect |
| Medium | Significant operational, worker, or customer effect |
| High | Serious legal, financial, safety, or mission effect |
| Critical | Severe harm, major legal exposure, worker exploitation, mission failure, or crisis |

## 3.3 Overall Risk

Overall risk may be rated:

- Low;
- Medium;
- High;
- Critical.

---

# 4. Escalation

Risks must be escalated when:

- overall risk is High or Critical;
- worker safety is involved;
- legal advice is needed;
- payroll or tax compliance is at risk;
- compensation concerns affect workers;
- customer contract exposure is significant;
- spiritual coercion or church boundary confusion appears;
- board decision is needed;
- mission alignment is compromised;
- financial sustainability is threatened.

# 6. Standard Cooperative Risks

The following standard risks should be considered when launching or reviewing cooperative ministry.

| Risk ID | Risk Description | Category | Default Review |
|---|---|---|---|
| COOP-RISK-MISSION-001 | Cooperative becomes merely commercial and loses ministry purpose | Mission Alignment | Annual |
| COOP-RISK-WORKER-001 | Workers are treated as low-cost mission labour rather than dignified participants | Worker Dignity | Quarterly |
| COOP-RISK-LEGAL-001 | Worker status is misclassified | Legal / Employment | Before launch and quarterly |
| COOP-RISK-PAY-001 | Compensation fails to comply with law or policy | Compensation | Quarterly |
| COOP-RISK-SAFETY-001 | Work exposes workers to preventable harm | Safety | Monthly |
| COOP-RISK-CONTRACT-001 | Contract creates obligations beyond cooperative capacity | Contracts | Before approval |
| COOP-RISK-CASH-001 | Cash flow cannot support payroll or obligations | Finance | Monthly |
| COOP-RISK-CHURCH-001 | Cooperative confuses work community with church authority | Church Boundary | Annual |
| COOP-RISK-COERCION-001 | Workers feel spiritual participation affects work standing | Spiritual Care | Quarterly |
| COOP-RISK-CONFLICT-001 | Related-party transactions or insider benefits distort decisions | Governance | Quarterly |
| COOP-RISK-REPORT-001 | Reporting failures hide operational or financial problems | Reporting | Monthly |

# 7. Risk Review Meeting

Cooperative risk should be reviewed at least quarterly during active operations and monthly during pilot launch.

A risk review meeting should ask:

- What new risks have emerged?
- Which risks increased?
- Which risks decreased?
- Which risks need escalation?
- Which risks need board decision?
- Which risks require policy revision?
- Which risks affect workers?
- Which risks affect mission alignment?
- Which risks require legal or professional advice?

---

# 8. Risk Mitigation

Mitigation may include:

- worker training;
- safety pause;
- legal review;
- contract revision;
- customer refusal;
- compensation correction;
- payroll review;
- reserve funding;
- insurance update;
- board decision;
- conflict management;
- chaplaincy clarification;
- reporting correction;
- manager training;
- mission alignment review;
- pause on scaling;
- pilot closure review.

---

# 9. Reporting

Risk summaries should be included in:

- [[Worker Cooperative Manager 5-15 Report]];
- monthly cooperative summary;
- board reports;
- [[Annual Cooperative Report]];
- [[Cooperative Mission Alignment Review]].

Reports should protect worker and customer confidentiality.

---

# 10. Closure

A risk may be closed when:

- the risk no longer exists;
- mitigation is complete;
- responsibility has transferred;
- the relevant project ended;
- board or cooperative authority approves closure.

Closure should be documented with reasons.

---

# 11. Review

This register should be reviewed quarterly during active cooperative operations and updated whenever significant incidents occur.
