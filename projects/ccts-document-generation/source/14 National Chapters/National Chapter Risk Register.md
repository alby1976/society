# National Chapter Risk Register

## 1. Purpose

This register provides a standard structure for tracking risks in National Chapters of the Christian Community Transformation Society.

National Chapter risks must be named, owned, reviewed, mitigated, and escalated. A chapter risk hidden in local conversation can become an international governance problem very quickly.

---

# 2. Risk Categories

National Chapter risks may include:

- doctrine and mission drift;
- weak chapter governance;
- failure to respect reserved powers;
- local adaptation without approval;
- legal noncompliance;
- charity or nonprofit filing failure;
- weak financial controls;
- restricted fund errors;
- donor influence;
- reporting failure;
- leadership overload;
- leadership misconduct;
- lack of qualified leaders;
- church relationship conflict;
- privacy breach;
- safeguarding concern;
- reputation risk;
- ministry overexpansion;
- unauthorized public communication;
- translation errors;
- conflict of interest;
- cross-border restrictions;
- national political or legal risk;
- chaplaincy boundary confusion;
- housing, cooperative, or foundation ministry risk.

---

# 3. Risk Rating

## 3.1 Likelihood

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Low | Unlikely |
| Medium | Possible |
| High | Likely |
| Urgent | Already happening or imminent |

## 3.2 Impact

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Low | Minor inconvenience or limited effect |
| Medium | Significant chapter, ministry, or governance effect |
| High | Serious legal, financial, doctrine, church, or mission effect |
| Critical | Severe harm, major legal exposure, public crisis, mission failure, or chapter suspension risk |

## 3.3 Overall Risk

Overall risk may be rated:

- Low;
- Medium;
- High;
- Critical.

---

# 4. Escalation

Risks must be escalated when:

- overall risk is High or Critical;
- doctrine or mission is involved;
- legal or charity compliance is involved;
- reserved powers may be breached;
- financial controls fail;
- safeguarding or privacy issues arise;
- church relationship conflict threatens mission;
- reporting failure continues;
- chapter recognition conditions are not met;
- public communications may create reputational risk;
- International Board decision is needed.

# 6. Standard National Chapter Risks

The following standard risks should be considered when launching or reviewing a National Chapter.

| Risk ID | Risk Description | Category | Default Review |
|---|---|---|---|
| CHAPTER-RISK-MISSION-001 | Chapter begins functioning as a separate mission | Mission Alignment | Annual |
| CHAPTER-RISK-DOCTRINE-001 | Chapter modifies or weakens doctrinal standards in practice | Doctrine | Annual |
| CHAPTER-RISK-RESERVED-001 | Chapter acts beyond delegated authority | Governance | Quarterly |
| CHAPTER-RISK-LEGAL-001 | Local legal obligations are missed | Legal | Quarterly |
| CHAPTER-RISK-FIN-001 | Financial controls are insufficient | Finance | Monthly |
| CHAPTER-RISK-REPORT-001 | Required reports are not submitted or reviewed | Reporting | Monthly |
| CHAPTER-RISK-LEADER-001 | Leaders are overloaded or unqualified for role | Leadership | Quarterly |
| CHAPTER-RISK-CHURCH-001 | Chapter confuses Society role with church authority | Church Relationship | Annual |
| CHAPTER-RISK-ADAPT-001 | Local adaptation changes core mission or governance | Local Adaptation | Every adaptation |
| CHAPTER-RISK-COMMS-001 | Public communication misrepresents chapter status or authority | Communications | Quarterly |
| CHAPTER-RISK-PRIVACY-001 | Sensitive information is mishandled | Privacy | Quarterly |

# 7. Risk Review Meeting

Chapter risk should be reviewed at least quarterly during active operations and monthly during formation or provisional launch.

A risk review meeting should ask:

- What new risks have emerged?
- Which risks increased?
- Which risks decreased?
- Which risks need escalation?
- Which risks need International Board attention?
- Which risks require legal review?
- Which risks require theological or church advisory review?
- Which risks affect recognition status?
- Which risks require policy revision?

---

# 8. Risk Mitigation

Mitigation may include:

- legal review;
- policy adoption;
- reporting improvement;
- leadership support;
- leadership replacement;
- financial controls;
- conflict of interest remedy;
- church advisory consultation;
- theological review;
- communication correction;
- ministry pause;
- local adaptation correction;
- Mission Alignment Review;
- recognition restriction;
- suspension review.

---

# 9. Reporting

Risk summaries should be included in:

- [[National Executive Director 5-15 Report]];
- [[National Board Summary]];
- International Board reports;
- [[Annual National Chapter Report]];
- [[National Chapter Mission Alignment Review]].

Reports should protect confidential personal, legal, financial, church, and ministry information.

---

# 10. Closure

A risk may be closed when:

- the risk no longer exists;
- mitigation is complete;
- responsibility has transferred;
- the relevant ministry or issue ended;
- board or International Society authority approves closure.

Closure should be documented with reasons.

---

# 11. Review

This register should be reviewed quarterly during active chapter operations and updated whenever significant incidents, legal matters, or recognition concerns arise.
