# Canada Legal and Charity Readiness Checklist

## 1. Purpose

This checklist helps the Canada Chapter formation team identify legal, charity, governance, financial, and operational readiness items before incorporation, charity registration, ministry launch, fundraising, housing activity, cooperative activity, or grant-making.

This document is not legal advice. It is a governance checklist to guide consultation with qualified Canadian legal, accounting, charity, employment, privacy, housing, and cooperative advisors where needed.

---

# 2. Governing Principle

No chapter should treat legal readiness as an afterthought.

A ministry can have a strong mission and still create serious problems if it forms the wrong legal structure, uses unclear objects, mishandles donations, overlooks employment obligations, or launches housing without proper review.
