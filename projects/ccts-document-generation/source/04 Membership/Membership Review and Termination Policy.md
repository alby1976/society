# Membership Review and Termination Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes how membership in the Christian Community Transformation Society is reviewed, renewed, resigned, suspended, transferred, or terminated.

The policy protects mission alignment, governance clarity, membership accuracy, and fair process.

---

# 2. Governing Principle

Membership should be meaningful enough to review and clear enough to end when appropriate.

The Society should not keep stale records, inactive governance roles, or unclear membership status. Fuzzy membership creates fuzzy authority, and fuzzy authority eventually eats the furniture.

---

# 3. Types of Review

Membership review may include:

- routine renewal review;
- eligibility review;
- category review;
- voting eligibility review;
- conduct review;
- conflict of interest review;
- mission alignment review;
- inactivity review;
- disciplinary review;
- national chapter transfer review.

---

# 4. Routine Renewal Review

Some membership categories may require renewal.

A renewal review may confirm:

- contact information;
- membership category;
- continued mission support;
- continued doctrinal affirmation or acknowledgment;
- conflict disclosure;
- local church information where appropriate;
- volunteer or service role;
- reporting obligations;
- privacy consent.

Renewal should be simple, but not meaningless.

---

# 5. Category Review

A member's category may be reviewed when:

- the member requests a change;
- the member becomes eligible for voting membership;
- the member no longer meets category requirements;
- the member moves countries;
- the member becomes staff, board member, volunteer, chaplain, or advisor;
- conflicts of interest change;
- national chapter status changes.

Category changes should be documented in the membership registry.

---

# 6. Inactivity Review

A member may be reviewed for inactivity if the member:

- cannot be contacted;
- has not participated for a defined period;
- has not renewed membership;
- has not fulfilled required reporting;
- has stopped supporting the mission;
- has moved away from the national chapter without transfer.

Inactivity review may result in:

- contact attempt;
- category change;
- suspension of voting rights;
- transfer to inactive status;
- termination of membership;
- archival of record.

---

# 7. Resignation

A member may resign by written notice or other approved method.

The Society should confirm resignation and update the membership registry.

Resignation does not remove obligations concerning:

- confidentiality;
- conflicts of interest;
- property;
- funds;
- reports;
- unresolved misconduct;
- legal matters;
- outstanding commitments.

---

# 8. Transfer

A member may transfer between national chapters where appropriate.

Transfer may require:

- confirmation of current status;
- receiving chapter approval;
- updated contact information;
- review of membership category;
- conflict disclosure;
- local church information if required;
- registry update.

International and national records should remain consistent.

---

# 9. Suspension

Membership may be suspended temporarily.

Reasons may include:

- pending disciplinary review;
- eligibility concern;
- unresolved conflict of interest;
- failure to renew;
- safeguarding concern;
- legal concern;
- mission alignment concern;
- misuse of Society name.

Suspension may include loss of voting rights, volunteer role, committee role, access to confidential records, or event participation.

Suspension is not necessarily termination.

---

# 10. Termination Grounds

Membership may be terminated for:

- resignation;
- death;
- failure to renew;
- failure to meet eligibility requirements;
- transfer out of applicable membership category;
- serious breach of [[Membership Covenant]];
- discipline outcome under [[Member Discipline Policy]];
- doctrinal rejection where affirmation is required;
- mission misalignment;
- misconduct;
- conflict of interest abuse;
- false application information;
- prolonged inactivity;
- legal or safeguarding concerns;
- dissolution of a national chapter where no transfer is available.

---

# 11. Termination Process

Termination should normally include:

1. identification of grounds;
2. review by authorized person or body;
3. notice where appropriate;
4. opportunity to respond where appropriate;
5. decision;
6. written record;
7. update to membership registry;
8. appeal information if applicable;
9. confidentiality protections.

For routine failure to renew, a simplified process may be used.

For serious misconduct, use [[Member Discipline Policy]].

---

# 12. Authority to Terminate

Authority to terminate membership should be defined by bylaws, policy, or the [[Authority Matrix]].

Possible authorities include:

- membership coordinator for administrative closure;
- National Executive Director;
- National Board;
- International Executive Director;
- International Board;
- membership committee;
- discipline panel.

Voting membership and leadership-related membership should require more formal process.

---

# 13. Appeal

A terminated member may appeal if allowed by policy.

Appeals may consider:

- procedural fairness;
- mistake of fact;
- new information;
- conflict of interest;
- disproportionate outcome;
- misapplication of membership standard.

Appeals should be timely and documented.

---

# 14. Recordkeeping

Membership review and termination records should be retained according to records policy.

Records may include:

- renewal notices;
- resignation notices;
- category changes;
- transfer records;
- suspension notices;
- termination decisions;
- appeal records;
- final status.

Membership registry should be updated promptly.

---

# 15. Communication

Communication about membership termination should be limited and appropriate.

The Society should not publicize membership termination unless necessary for governance, safety, law, or correction of public confusion.

Where a person's role is public, a simple statement may be needed.

---

# 16. Restoration or Reapplication

A former member may reapply unless barred by discipline, law, or policy.

Reapplication may require:

- waiting period;
- evidence of changed circumstances;
- restoration plan completion;
- board or membership committee approval;
- renewed covenant affirmation;
- updated conflict disclosure.

Restoration should be hopeful but not naive.

---
