# National Board Summary

## Template Instructions

This template is for quarterly or periodic summaries prepared for a National Board or by a National Board for the International Society.

It should summarize chapter-level governance, finances, risks, ministry activity, church relationships, mission alignment, and decisions needed.

This summary does not replace formal board minutes.
