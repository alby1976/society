# Capacity Building Grants

## Executive Summary

Capacity-Building Grants support ministries, churches, leaders, or institutions by increasing their long-term ability to serve faithfully, sustainably, and in alignment with the Society's mission.

## Adopted Principles

- Grants should develop durable capacity, not merely fund short-term activity.
- Grants should have measurable outcomes and reporting requirements.
- Grants should preserve theological and mission alignment.
- Grants should be reviewed for stewardship, sustainability, and church benefit.

## Possible Uses

- Leadership development.
- Ministry training.
- Administrative systems.
- Church support infrastructure.
- Housing or cooperative feasibility work.
- Seminary or theological education support connected to ministry commitment.

## Key Decisions

- Capacity-building grants are preferred within the Foundation framework.
- Grant policy should include accountability if funded ministry commitments are abandoned.
