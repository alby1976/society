# National Chapter Board Charter

## 1. Purpose

This charter defines the role, authority, responsibilities, limits, and accountability of a National Chapter Board within the Christian Community Transformation Society.

A National Chapter Board governs the Society's recognized work within its country or jurisdiction under the International Constitution, International Governance Framework, reserved powers, delegated authority, and mission alignment standards.

---

# 2. Governing Principle

A National Chapter Board is a steward of a received mission, not the owner of an independent mission.

The Board should govern locally with wisdom, legal compliance, and contextual awareness while remaining accountable to the Society's international doctrine, mission, and governance standards.

Local responsibility is real. International accountability is also real.

---

# 3. Authority

The National Chapter Board receives authority through:

- the International Constitution;
- National Chapter recognition;
- delegated authority from the International Board;
- local legal documents;
- approved chapter bylaws or equivalent;
- written policies;
- applicable local law.

The Board may not act beyond its delegated authority or violate reserved powers.

---

# 4. Core Responsibilities

The National Chapter Board is responsible to:

1. preserve doctrine and mission alignment;
2. ensure legal compliance in the jurisdiction;
3. oversee the National Executive Director or equivalent;
4. approve national budgets;
5. oversee financial stewardship;
6. manage risk;
7. approve national policies where delegated;
8. ensure reporting to the International Society;
9. maintain church-supporting identity;
10. oversee national ministries;
11. protect institutional memory;
12. manage conflicts of interest;
13. ensure proper records;
14. review annual reports;
15. participate in Mission Alignment Review.

---

# 5. Limits of Authority

A National Chapter Board may not independently:

- alter the Statement of Faith;
- alter the Society's mission, vision, or core values;
- amend international reserved powers;
- represent itself as independent from the International Society;
- launch major ministries requiring international approval;
- acquire or dispose of major assets beyond authority;
- create doctrinal standards inconsistent with the Society;
- use the Society's name contrary to policy;
- override Theological Oversight where applicable;
- control local churches;
- replace the local church.

---

# 6. Board Composition

The Board should be composed of qualified persons with capacity for:

- governance;
- financial oversight;
- mission understanding;
- church-supporting discernment;
- legal and compliance awareness;
- risk management;
- local cultural understanding;
- prayerful leadership.

Composition must comply with local law and international standards.

---

# 7. Qualifications

Board members should demonstrate:

- integrity;
- commitment to the Society's mission;
- willingness to uphold the Statement of Faith where required;
- respect for local churches;
- ability to maintain confidentiality;
- freedom from unmanaged conflicts;
- willingness to submit to governance;
- capacity to read reports and make decisions;
- prayerful seriousness.

A person should not serve merely because they are enthusiastic, available, influential, or related to someone important.

---

# 8. Duties of Care, Loyalty, and Obedience

Board members should act with reasonable attention, preparation, prudence, and diligence.

They should be loyal to the Society's mission rather than personal, family, church, donor, business, or political interests.

They should obey local law, governing documents, reserved powers, delegated authority, and mission standards.

---

# 9. Relationship to International Board

The National Chapter Board shall remain accountable to the International Board according to approved governance documents.

The International Board may require:

- reports;
- Mission Alignment Review;
- corrective action;
- legal documentation;
- financial information;
- leadership review;
- ministry pause;
- policy adoption;
- risk escalation;
- annual confirmation.

---

# 10. Committees

The Board may establish committees such as:

- finance committee;
- governance committee;
- risk committee;
- housing committee;
- cooperative committee;
- foundation committee;
- church advisory liaison;
- audit or review committee;
- nominating committee.

Committees require written terms of reference and may not exceed delegated authority.

---

# 11. Meetings

The Board should meet regularly according to local law and chapter bylaws.

Meetings should include:

- agenda;
- prayer where appropriate;
- conflict declaration;
- minutes;
- decision records;
- financial review;
- risk review;
- ministry reports;
- action items;
- follow-up.

Meetings should not be merely ceremonial. A board that does not decide, review, or correct is not governing.

---

# 12. Financial Oversight

The Board shall oversee:

- annual budget;
- financial statements;
- bank controls;
- restricted funds;
- grant funds;
- expense approvals;
- reserves;
- audit or review requirements;
- tax filings;
- donor restrictions;
- financial risk.

---

# 13. Risk Oversight

The Board shall ensure that risks are identified, recorded, reviewed, and escalated.

Risks may include doctrinal drift, legal noncompliance, financial weakness, safeguarding concerns, privacy issues, church relationship concerns, ministry overexpansion, leadership burnout, reporting failure, and reputational harm.

---

# 14. Reporting

The Board shall submit or approve required reports, including:

- [[National Board Summary]];
- financial reports;
- ministry summaries;
- risk reports;
- annual ministry summary;
- Mission Alignment Review;
- document registry updates;
- decision register updates.

---

# 15. Confidentiality

Board members must protect confidential information, including personnel matters, resident information, worker information, grant applications, donor records, legal advice, church concerns, financial details, safeguarding matters, and strategic decisions.

---

# 16. Conflicts of Interest

Board members must disclose and manage conflicts according to [[Conflict of Interest Policy]].

Conflicted members may need to leave discussion, abstain from voting, or be excluded from documents.

---

# 17. Board Review

The Board should review its own performance annually.

Review should ask:

- Are we preserving mission?
- Are we submitting reports?
- Are we reading reports?
- Are we managing risk?
- Are we correcting problems?
- Are we respecting churches?
- Are we staying within authority?
- Are we protecting institutional memory?

---

# 18. Review

This charter should be reviewed annually during chapter launch and at least every three years once stable.
