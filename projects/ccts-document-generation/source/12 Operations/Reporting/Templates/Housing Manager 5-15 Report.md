# Housing Manager 5-15 Report

## Template Instructions

This template is for housing managers, property stewards, resident support coordinators, or leaders responsible for Society-related housing.

Housing reports may involve sensitive resident, family, financial, lease, maintenance, safety, or pastoral information. Use the proper confidentiality level and avoid unnecessary identifying details.

This report should help leaders understand housing stability, resident support needs, property concerns, community life, financial risks, church connection, and decisions needed.
