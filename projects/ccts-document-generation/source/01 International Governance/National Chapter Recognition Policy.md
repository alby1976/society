# National Chapter Recognition Policy

## Purpose

To define how a national chapter is assessed, approved, launched, supervised, reviewed, suspended, or dissolved.

## Adopted Principles

- New chapters begin with prayer, church relationships, legal review, and governance readiness.
- Recognition is a privilege, not an entitlement.
- Chapters must subscribe to international theology and mission.
- Chapters must show local capacity and accountability.

## Key Decisions

- Each chapter requires an approved national charter.
- Each chapter must maintain alignment with the international Society.
- Recognition may be suspended for doctrinal, legal, financial, or governance failure.

## International Application

This document is written for an international society. National chapters may localize procedures required by civil law, but they may not alter the Society's theology, mission, reserved powers, or core governance identity without approval under the international governance framework.
