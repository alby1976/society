# Governance Architecture

## Purpose

This document describes the high-level governance architecture of the Christian Community Transformation Society as an international society.

It helps board members, chapter leaders, advisors, staff, volunteers, churches, and future leaders understand how the Society is organized and how its documents relate to one another.

## Christ and Scripture

The Society's highest allegiance is to Jesus Christ. Scripture is the supreme written authority for the Society's faith and life.

No constitution, bylaw, policy, board decision, national chapter practice, grant, housing program, cooperative, or ministry may knowingly contradict Scripture.

## Theological Foundation

The [[Statement of Faith]] governs the Society's doctrinal identity.

The Society is confessionally Reformed and church-supporting. It is not a church and does not replace local church authority.

## Mission and Vision Foundation

The [[Mission Statement]] defines what the Society does.

The [[Vision Statement]] defines what the Society longs to see.

The [[Core Values]] define the character and commitments that should shape the Society's work.

## International Society

The Society is international by design.

The international structure protects doctrine, mission, identity, reserved powers, chapter recognition, international expansion, and institutional continuity.

Canada may serve as the founding chapter, but Canada is not the theological center of the Society. The Society's center is Christ and its governing doctrine.

## National Chapters

National chapters operate under the international Society.

Each chapter complies with local law, implements the Society's mission locally, remains accountable to international doctrine and governance, maintains local board and operational structures as required, and reports to the international structure according to policy.

## Advisory and Oversight Bodies

### International Church Advisory Fellowship

Provides churchly counsel and cooperation without replacing local church authority.

### Theological Oversight Council

Reviews theological consistency, mission drift, doctrinal questions, and ministry concerns.

### Stewardship Advisors

Preserve institutional memory and advise future leaders without exercising governing authority.

## Ministry Structures

The Society may operate or oversee ministries such as Housing Trust, Worker Cooperative, Church Support Foundation, Chaplaincy, leadership development, community ministries, prayer gatherings, education, and training.

Each ministry must remain aligned with the Society's mission and theology.
