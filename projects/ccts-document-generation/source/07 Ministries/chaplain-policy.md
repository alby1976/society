# Chaplain Policy

## Executive Summary

The Society Chaplain provides spiritual care, prayer leadership, devotional support, mediation, and connection to local churches. The chaplain serves the Society, not merely the worker cooperative, and must not replace local pastors or contradict church leadership.

## Adopted Principles

- The chaplain belongs to the Society's ministry framework.
- The chaplain provides temporary spiritual care for unchurched people with the goal of connecting them to faithful local churches.
- The chaplain may facilitate the Annual Day of Prayer.
- The chaplain may provide devotional meditation during the Annual Day of Prayer.
- The chaplain must not contradict local church leadership.
- The chaplain must not replace ministers of the Word, elders, sacraments, or church discipline.

## Qualifications

The chaplain should have:

- Credible Christian character.
- Theological training.
- Training from a seminary recognized by the Church Advisory Fellowship.
- Accountability within the Society's governance framework.
- Respect for the authority of local churches.

## Key Decisions

- The chaplain serves the Society rather than the worker cooperative alone.
- Seminary recognition is connected to the Church Advisory Fellowship.
- The chaplain can support the Annual Day of Prayer.
