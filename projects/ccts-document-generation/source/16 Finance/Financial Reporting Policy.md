# Financial Reporting Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes standards for financial reports within the Christian Community Transformation Society.

Financial reporting allows boards, leaders, ministries, chapters, donors where appropriate, and future leaders to understand how funds were received, restricted, spent, reserved, and stewarded.

---

# 2. Governing Principle

Financial reports should be accurate, timely, understandable, and reviewed.

A report that no one reads does not provide oversight. A report that no one understands does not provide clarity.

---

# 3. Scope

This policy applies to:

- international financial reports;
- national chapter financial reports;
- ministry financial reports;
- restricted fund reports;
- grant reports;
- housing reports;
- cooperative reports;
- Foundation reports;
- annual reports;
- board finance reports.

---

# 4. Standard Reports

The Society may use the following financial reports:

- budget-to-actual report;
- income and expense report;
- balance report;
- cash flow summary;
- restricted fund report;
- grant disbursement report;
- reserve report;
- expense report;
- donor restricted fund summary;
- annual financial summary.

---

# 5. Reporting Frequency

Recommended reporting frequency:

| Report | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Budget-to-actual | Monthly or quarterly |
| Restricted fund report | Monthly or quarterly |
| Ministry financial summary | Monthly or quarterly |
| National Chapter financial report | Monthly or quarterly |
| Foundation grant report | Quarterly |
| Housing financial report | Monthly or quarterly |
| Cooperative financial report | Monthly |
| Annual financial summary | Annual |

Frequency may increase during launch, risk, or corrective action.

---

# 6. Report Contents

A financial report should include:

- reporting period;
- prepared by;
- reviewed by;
- income;
- expenses;
- budget comparison;
- restricted funds;
- reserves;
- major variances;
- concerns;
- decisions needed;
- follow-up items.

---

# 7. Restricted Fund Reporting

Restricted fund reports should identify:

- opening balance;
- gifts received;
- expenses;
- transfers;
- closing balance;
- purpose;
- restrictions;
- reporting obligations;
- unused funds;
- concerns.

Restricted funds should not be hidden in general totals.

---

# 8. Variance Reporting

Material variances should be explained.

A variance note should include:

- budget line;
- expected amount;
- actual amount;
- difference;
- reason;
- action needed;
- whether board approval is required.

---

# 9. Review Responsibility

Financial reports should be reviewed by the appropriate authority.

Possible reviewers:

- Treasurer;
- Finance Committee;
- National Chapter Board;
- International Board;
- Foundation Committee;
- Housing Committee;
- Cooperative Board;
- Executive Director;
- external accountant or auditor.

Review should be documented.

---

# 10. Financial Report Red Flags

Red flags include:

- missing reports;
- unexplained variances;
- negative cash flow;
- restricted fund confusion;
- missing receipts;
- late filings;
- unapproved expenses;
- related-party payments;
- grant reporting failures;
- excessive dependence on one donor;
- lack of reserves;
- unclear payroll or contractor payments.

Red flags should be escalated.

---

# 11. Confidentiality

Financial reports may include sensitive information.

Reports should protect:

- donor privacy;
- employee or contractor compensation details;
- grant recipient information;
- resident information;
- student support information;
- legal matters;
- restricted fund details where confidential.

---

# 12. Annual Financial Summary

An annual financial summary should be prepared for institutional memory.

It should include:

- total income;
- total expenses;
- major funds;
- restricted fund summary;
- reserves;
- major decisions;
- major risks;
- audit or review status;
- lessons learned;
- goals for next year.

---

# 13. Audit or External Review

Where required or wise, the Society should obtain external accounting, review, or audit support.

External review may be required by:

- law;
- bylaws;
- donor restriction;
- board decision;
- grant condition;
- risk concern;
- national chapter status.

---

# 14. Recordkeeping

Financial reports should be stored according to [[Records Management Policy]].

Financial records should be retained according to local law and Society retention standards.

---

# 15. Review

This policy should be reviewed annually with the budget cycle and after any major financial concern.
