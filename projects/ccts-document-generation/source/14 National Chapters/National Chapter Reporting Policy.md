# National Chapter Reporting Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes reporting requirements for National Chapters of the Christian Community Transformation Society.

Reporting exists to preserve mission alignment, support governance, identify risks early, maintain institutional memory, and prevent future leaders from guessing what happened.

---

# 2. Governing Principle

A chapter that does not report cannot be responsibly governed.

Reports should be useful, truthful, concise, timely, and proportionate. Reporting should not become busywork, but silence is not stewardship.

---

# 3. Scope

This policy applies to:

- National Chapter Boards;
- National Executive Directors;
- ministry leaders;
- chaplains;
- housing managers;
- cooperative managers;
- foundation directors;
- finance officers;
- committees;
- provisional chapters;
- recognized chapters;
- restricted or suspended chapters.

---

# 4. Required Report Types

A National Chapter may be required to submit:

- [[National Executive Director 5-15 Report]];
- [[National Board Summary]];
- ministry 5-15 reports;
- financial reports;
- risk reports;
- annual ministry summary;
- Mission Alignment Review;
- chapter recognition confirmation;
- document registry updates;
- decision register updates;
- incident reports where applicable.

---

# 5. Reporting Frequency

Reporting frequency should be based on chapter status.

| Chapter Status | Reporting Frequency |
|---|---|
| Formation Team | Monthly or as required |
| Provisional Chapter | Monthly |
| Recognized Chapter | Monthly or quarterly, plus annual |
| Restricted Chapter | As required by corrective action |
| Suspended Chapter | As required by International Board |

Ministry-level reports may be weekly, monthly, quarterly, or annual depending on role and risk.

---

# 6. National Executive Reporting

The National Executive Director should submit reports using [[National Executive Director 5-15 Report]].

Reports should include:

- chapter health;
- ministry updates;
- finances;
- risks;
- church relationships;
- legal compliance;
- staffing;
- support needed;
- decisions required;
- prayer requests.

---

# 7. Board Reporting

The National Chapter Board should prepare [[National Board Summary]] reports.

Board summaries should include:

- governance decisions;
- financial oversight;
- ministry review;
- risks;
- policy updates;
- conflicts of interest;
- mission alignment concerns;
- items for International Society attention.

---

# 8. Financial Reporting

Financial reports should include:

- income;
- expenses;
- budget comparison;
- restricted funds;
- bank balances;
- grants;
- major liabilities;
- reserves;
- donor restrictions;
- audit or review status;
- tax or filing obligations.

Financial reporting must comply with local law and Society standards.

---

# 9. Ministry Reporting

Each active ministry should report using the relevant template.

Examples:

- [[Chaplain 5-15 Report]];
- [[Housing Manager 5-15 Report]];
- [[Worker Cooperative Manager 5-15 Report]];
- [[Foundation Director 5-15 Report]];
- ministry leader reports;
- annual ministry reports.

---

# 10. Risk Reporting

National Chapters shall report significant risks promptly.

Risks may involve:

- doctrine;
- mission;
- finances;
- legal compliance;
- safeguarding;
- privacy;
- church relationships;
- public reputation;
- property;
- grants;
- worker safety;
- leadership capacity.

High risks should not wait for the next scheduled report.

---

# 11. Incident Reporting

Serious incidents must be escalated promptly.

Incidents may include:

- safeguarding concern;
- serious financial concern;
- legal notice;
- death or serious harm connected to ministry;
- privacy breach;
- public controversy;
- leadership misconduct;
- serious church relationship issue;
- major property incident;
- grant misuse.

---

# 12. Annual Reporting

Each National Chapter should submit an annual ministry summary.

The annual summary should preserve:

- major decisions;
- ministries conducted;
- people served;
- financial stewardship;
- risks;
- church relationships;
- Mission Alignment Review;
- lessons learned;
- future goals;
- prayer requests.

---

# 13. Report Review

Reports should be reviewed by the proper authority.

Reviewers should identify:

- decisions needed;
- risks requiring escalation;
- missed reports;
- mission alignment concerns;
- financial issues;
- follow-up tasks;
- policy gaps;
- institutional memory items.

Reports should not simply be filed unread.

---

# 14. Confidentiality

Reports may contain sensitive information.

Confidential details should be minimized and restricted to appropriate audiences.

Reports should protect:

- residents;
- workers;
- students;
- donors;
- grant applicants;
- staff;
- volunteers;
- churches;
- legal matters;
- safeguarding concerns.

---

# 15. Failure to Report

Failure to report may result in:

- reminder;
- support offered;
- reporting simplification;
- corrective action;
- increased oversight;
- restriction of authority;
- pause on ministry;
- suspension review.

The goal is correction, not punishment. But repeated silence cannot be ignored.

---

# 16. Institutional Memory

Reports should be stored according to [[Institutional Memory Policy]].

Reports help future leaders understand:

- why decisions were made;
- what worked;
- what failed;
- what risks emerged;
- what God provided;
- what must not be forgotten.

---

# 17. Review

This policy should be reviewed annually during international formation and at least every three years once chapter reporting is stable.
