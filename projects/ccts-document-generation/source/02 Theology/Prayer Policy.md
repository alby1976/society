# Prayer Policy

## Purpose

To establish prayer as a required practice throughout the founding, governance, expansion, and ministry life of the Society.

## Adopted Principles

- Prayer is not decorative governance furniture.
- Prayer should precede each major founding stage.
- Annual prayer should renew dependence on God.
- Chapters should pray before expansion, incorporation, major partnerships, and ministry launch.

## Key Decisions

- Prayer is required before stages 2 through 12 of the founding roadmap.
- The chaplain may facilitate the Annual Day of Prayer and give a devotional meditation.
- Prayer practices must not replace local church worship.

## International Application

This document is written for an international society. National chapters may localize procedures required by civil law, but they may not alter the Society's theology, mission, reserved powers, or core governance identity without approval under the international governance framework.
