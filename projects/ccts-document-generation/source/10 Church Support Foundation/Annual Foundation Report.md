# Annual Foundation Report

## Template Instructions

This template is used to prepare an annual report for the Church Support Foundation, a national chapter foundation, a restricted grant fund, or a Foundation-related grant program.

The report should summarize grant activity, stewardship, donor restrictions, recipient accountability, church relationships, student support, capacity-building, risks, mission alignment, major decisions, lessons learned, and prayer needs.

The report should be preserved as part of institutional memory.
