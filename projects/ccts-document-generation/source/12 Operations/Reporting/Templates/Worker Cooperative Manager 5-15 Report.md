# Worker Cooperative Manager 5-15 Report

## Template Instructions

This template is for managers, coordinators, or leaders responsible for a Society-affiliated worker cooperative or cooperative pilot.

The report should help leaders understand cooperative operations, worker development, member health, business risks, mission alignment, financial concerns, discipleship connection, and decisions needed.

Do not include unnecessary confidential employment, financial, member, or client details.
