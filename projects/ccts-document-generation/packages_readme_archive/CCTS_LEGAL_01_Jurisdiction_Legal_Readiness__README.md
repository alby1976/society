# CCTS LEGAL-01 — Jurisdiction Legal Readiness

This generator-ready source package adds legal readiness and jurisdiction review tools for the Christian Community Transformation Society.

## Contents

- `source/20 Legal Readiness/Legal Readiness Framework.md`
- `source/20 Legal Readiness/Jurisdiction Legal Readiness Checklist.md`
- `source/20 Legal Readiness/Legal Counsel Engagement Brief.md`
- `source/20 Legal Readiness/Legal Issue Register.md`
- `source/90 Institutional Memory/Registry Updates/Document Registry Update - LEGAL-01.md`

## Legal note

These documents are governance tools, not legal advice. They are designed to help the Society know when to seek qualified legal advice and how to preserve legal readiness records.

## Metadata note

Following the updated YAML rule, `scriptural-foundation` and `confessional-foundation` fields are left blank in these legal-readiness documents because they do not directly depend on a specific Scripture passage or confessional article as their document foundation.

## Use

Copy the included `source/` folder into your Python content generator project, merging it with existing source files.

Then run:

```bash
python build.py
```

Edit source files only in `source/`, not in generated `dist/` output.
