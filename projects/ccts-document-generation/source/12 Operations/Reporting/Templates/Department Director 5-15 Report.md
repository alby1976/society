# Department Director 5-15 Report

## Template Instructions

This template is for department directors or senior ministry managers responsible for a department, operational area, or multiple ministry teams.

The report should focus on department objectives, team health, budget concerns, risks, decisions needed, mission alignment, and cross-functional coordination.

Use concise summaries. Attach or link detailed reports only when needed.
