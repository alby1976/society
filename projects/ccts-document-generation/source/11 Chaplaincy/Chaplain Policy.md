# Chaplain Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the role, authority, responsibilities, limits, reporting duties, confidentiality expectations, and accountability of a chaplain serving the Christian Community Transformation Society.

The chaplain provides spiritual care within the Society's mission while respecting the authority of faithful local churches.

---

# 2. Governing Principle

The chaplain serves under the Society's authority and in support of the local church.

The chaplain is not the pastor of a Society-church, because the Society is not a church.

The chaplain's ministry should strengthen spiritual care, encourage prayer, support vulnerable people, and help connect persons to faithful church life where appropriate.

---

# 3. Scope of Chaplaincy

The chaplain may serve:

- Society staff;
- volunteers;
- workers in Society-affiliated cooperatives;
- housing residents;
- grant or scholarship recipients where appropriate;
- beneficiaries;
- families connected to Society ministries;
- national chapter leaders;
- ministry leaders;
- persons who are unchurched and receiving Society support;
- persons in crisis connected to Society ministries.

---

# 4. Core Duties

A chaplain may be responsible to:

1. provide spiritual care;
2. pray with and for persons connected to Society ministries;
3. offer biblical encouragement;
4. support ministry leaders in spiritual care concerns;
5. support the Annual Day of Prayer;
6. provide devotional meditation when authorized;
7. assist with church referrals;
8. advise on spiritual care themes;
9. participate in crisis response;
10. maintain confidentiality;
11. submit reports;
12. identify risks requiring escalation;
13. remain doctrinally aligned;
14. respect local church leadership.

---

# 5. Spiritual Care Activities

Spiritual care may include:

- prayer;
- Scripture reading;
- listening;
- encouragement;
- grief support;
- crisis support;
- referral to local church;
- referral to professional help where appropriate;
- devotional support;
- support during major life transitions;
- support during housing, employment, or family stress.

The chaplain should recognize the limits of the role and refer matters outside competence.

---

# 6. Local Church Boundary

The chaplain must not contradict local church leadership.

Where a person is a member of a faithful local church, the chaplain should avoid replacing that church's pastoral oversight.

The chaplain may encourage the person to speak with their pastor, elder, or appropriate church officer.

Where appropriate and authorized, the chaplain may coordinate with local church leadership.

---

# 7. Care for Unchurched Persons

The chaplain may temporarily provide spiritual care to unchurched persons connected to Society ministries.

The aim should ordinarily be to help the person connect to a faithful local church, not to create permanent dependence on the Society's chaplaincy.

The chaplain should exercise patience, wisdom, and compassion.

---

# 8. Devotional Teaching

The chaplain may provide devotional teaching or meditation when authorized.

Devotional teaching must:

- be consistent with [[Statement of Faith]];
- avoid contradiction of local church leadership;
- avoid replacing Lord's Day preaching;
- remain appropriate to the Society's role;
- be accountable to theological review where needed.

---

# 9. Annual Day of Prayer

The chaplain may facilitate or assist with the Society's Annual Day of Prayer.

The chaplain may:

- help prepare prayer themes;
- coordinate prayer requests;
- lead portions of prayer where appropriate;
- provide a devotional meditation;
- encourage thanksgiving, confession, supplication, and intercession;
- help connect prayer concerns to the Society's mission.

The Annual Day of Prayer must not become a substitute for Lord's Day worship.

---

# 10. Confidentiality

The chaplain shall maintain confidentiality according to Society policy and applicable law.

The chaplain should not disclose sensitive information except where:

- the person consents;
- law requires disclosure;
- safety is at risk;
- safeguarding concerns arise;
- serious harm may occur;
- governance authority requires limited disclosure;
- referral or support requires disclosure;
- policy requires escalation.

The chaplain should not promise absolute confidentiality.

---

# 11. Reporting

The chaplain shall submit reports according to the Society's reporting framework.

Reports should normally use [[Chaplain 5-15 Report]].

Reports should include:

- activity summary;
- general themes;
- referrals;
- risks;
- prayer themes;
- support needed;
- decisions needed.

Reports should avoid unnecessary identifying details.

---

# 12. Supervision and Accountability

The chaplain shall be accountable to the authority defined in the role description.

Possible accountability structures include:

- National Executive Director;
- International Executive Director;
- designated chaplain supervisor;
- National Board;
- International Board;
- Church Advisory Fellowship for counsel;
- Theological Oversight Council for doctrinal review.

Accountability should be clear before appointment.

---

# 13. Conflicts of Interest

The chaplain must disclose conflicts of interest.

Examples:

- serving as pastor of a beneficiary's church;
- counseling a family member;
- involvement in housing decisions;
- involvement in employment decisions;
- grant or financial relationship;
- personal conflict with a person receiving care.

Conflicts should be managed carefully.

---

# 14. Safeguarding and Crisis

The chaplain must follow safeguarding and crisis policies.

The chaplain should escalate concerns involving:

- abuse or exploitation;
- immediate safety risk;
- self-neglect or serious vulnerability;
- violence risk;
- child or vulnerable adult concern;
- serious mental health crisis;
- legal reporting obligation;
- serious misconduct by Society personnel.

The chaplain should not attempt to handle every crisis alone.

---

# 15. Professional Boundaries

The chaplain must maintain appropriate boundaries.

The chaplain should avoid:

- dependency;
- favoritism;
- secretive relationships;
- dual relationships without disclosure;
- financial entanglement;
- inappropriate emotional control;
- unauthorized counseling beyond competence;
- acting as legal, medical, or financial professional.

---

# 16. Removal or Suspension

A chaplain may be suspended or removed for:

- doctrinal misalignment;
- breach of confidentiality;
- serious misconduct;
- boundary violation;
- refusal of accountability;
- safeguarding concern;
- incompetence;
- mission drift;
- contradiction of local church leadership;
- failure to report;
- unmanaged conflict of interest.

Removal should be documented and handled with appropriate confidentiality.

---
