# Society-Wide Risk Register

## 1. Purpose

This register provides the Society-wide structure for tracking major risks across the Christian Community Transformation Society.

Specialized registers may track ministry-level risks, but major risks should be summarized here for board oversight.

---

# 2. Register Instructions

Use this register for risks that affect:

- international mission;
- doctrine;
- governance;
- national chapters;
- finances;
- legal compliance;
- public trust;
- safety;
- privacy;
- church relationships;
- major ministries;
- leadership stability;
- institutional memory.

Do not record unnecessary confidential details. Link to restricted records where needed.

# 6. Quarterly Risk Review

Quarterly review should ask:

- Which risks increased?
- Which risks decreased?
- Which risks need escalation?
- Which risks need board decision?
- Which risks require Mission Alignment Review?
- Which risks require policy revision?
- Which risks reveal training needs?
- Which risks should be closed?
- Which risks should be added to annual report?

---

# 7. Review

This register should be reviewed quarterly and whenever a major incident or mission alignment concern arises.
