# Authority Matrix

## Purpose

This Authority Matrix summarizes who may approve, recommend, review, or implement major governance and operational decisions within the Christian Community Transformation Society.

This matrix is a working governance tool. It does not replace the [[International Constitution]], [[Reserved Powers Framework]], or [[Delegated Authority Framework]].

Where this matrix conflicts with a higher governing document, the higher document controls.

# Financial Authority Thresholds

The Society should adopt numerical thresholds later by jurisdiction and stage of development.

Until thresholds are adopted, the following principle applies:

| Decision Type | Default Authority |
|---|---|
| Routine approved budget expense | Executive authority within approved budget |
| Expense outside approved budget | Board approval |
| Major long-term obligation | Board approval |
| Debt or guarantee | Board approval; international review if cross-border |
| Property purchase or sale | Board approval; international review if mission-significant |
| Restricted gift with major obligations | Board approval |
| International asset transfer | International Board approval |

# Delegation Notes

## International Board

The International Board holds reserved powers and international constitutional authority.

## National Board

A National Board governs local implementation within delegated authority and local law.

## Executive Director

An Executive Director executes approved policy and strategy. Executive authority should be real but bounded.

## Theological Oversight Council

The Council usually recommends and reviews. It does not ordinarily govern unless a charter gives specific authority.

## Church Advisory Fellowship

The Fellowship advises and counsels. It does not replace church courts or board governance.

## Stewardship Advisors

Stewardship Advisors preserve memory and advise. They do not govern unless separately appointed to governing office.
