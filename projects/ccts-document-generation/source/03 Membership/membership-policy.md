# Membership Policy

## Executive Summary

Membership in the Society is a governance and mission-alignment relationship. Members should support the Society's theological identity, mission, and boundaries.

## Adopted Principles

- Membership should preserve the mission and theological identity of the Society.
- Membership may include multiple classes.
- Voting authority should be reserved for members aligned with the Society's doctrine and mission.
- Membership may be removed where a person no longer supports the Society's mission or violates adopted standards.

## Possible Membership Classes

- Founding Members.
- Voting Members.
- Supporting Members.
- Church Members.
- Honorary Members.

## Key Decisions

- Membership requires agreement with the Society's mission.
- Membership design should protect against future mission drift.
