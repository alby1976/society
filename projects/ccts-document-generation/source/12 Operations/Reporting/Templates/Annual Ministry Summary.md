# Annual Ministry Summary

## Template Instructions

This template is for annual ministry summaries prepared by ministries, national chapters, affiliated organizations, or the International Society.

Annual summaries should draw from 5-15 reports, monthly summaries, quarterly reports, financial reports, risk reports, board reports, and mission alignment reviews.

This report should be preserved permanently as part of institutional memory.
