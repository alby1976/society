# Worker Cooperative Framework

## Executive Summary

The Worker Cooperative is envisioned as a ministry-supporting enterprise that provides meaningful work, skills development, discipleship, and sustainable service under the Society's mission and theological oversight.

## Adopted Principles

- The cooperative serves the Society's mission.
- Work is part of Christian stewardship and discipleship.
- A pilot cooperative should precede larger institutional development.
- The cooperative should remain accountable to the Society's theological and governance boundaries.

## Key Decisions

- Launch a pilot cooperative before establishing the Church Support Foundation.
- Leadership development should happen before cooperative launch.
- A company such as We Care Consulting Inc. may eventually donate shares or transition ownership to the Society if legally and missionally appropriate.
