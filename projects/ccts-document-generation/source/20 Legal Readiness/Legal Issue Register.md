# Legal Issue Register

## 1. Purpose

This register tracks material legal questions, legal review items, legal risks, and legal decisions for the Society.

It helps prevent legal issues from being forgotten, handled informally, or repeated across chapters.

---

# 2. Register Instructions

Use this register for legal issues involving:

- incorporation;
- charity registration;
- bylaws;
- reserved powers;
- national chapters;
- fundraising;
- donations;
- grants;
- housing;
- employment;
- contractors;
- volunteers;
- privacy;
- safeguarding;
- cooperative activity;
- contracts;
- leases;
- public communications;
- cross-border transfers;
- insurance.

Do not include privileged legal advice in broadly accessible versions.

# 5. Review Rhythm

The Legal Issue Register should be reviewed:

- quarterly;
- before board decisions on legal matters;
- before national chapter recognition;
- before public fundraising;
- before housing launch;
- before grant-making;
- before cooperative launch;
- after legal advice is received.

---

# 6. Confidentiality

Legal issues may involve privileged or confidential information.

The register may record that legal advice exists without quoting the advice.

Sensitive legal documents should be stored in restricted locations.

---

# 7. Closure

An issue may be closed when:

- advice received;
- decision made;
- action completed;
- risk accepted;
- issue no longer applies;
- matter moved to corrective action or risk register.

Closure should record the reason.

---

# 8. Review

This register should be reviewed quarterly and after any major legal issue.
