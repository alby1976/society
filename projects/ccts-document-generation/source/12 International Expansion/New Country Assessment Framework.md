# New Country Assessment Framework

## Purpose

To assess whether a potential country is ready for chapter development.

## Adopted Principles

- Readiness includes theological, legal, financial, leadership, church relationship, and operational capacity.
- Desire to expand is not enough.
- Legal and cultural conditions must be understood before public launch.

## Key Decisions

- Each candidate country should complete a readiness brief before chapter formation.
- High-risk jurisdictions require additional review.

## International Application

This document is written for an international society. National chapters may localize procedures required by civil law, but they may not alter the Society's theology, mission, reserved powers, or core governance identity without approval under the international governance framework.
