# Vision Statement

## Vision

Communities transformed by the Gospel as faithful churches equip believers to glorify God in every area of life throughout the week.

## Adopted Principles

- The Society seeks long-term community transformation rooted in faithful Christian worship, discipleship, service, and stewardship.
- The Society does not measure success merely by programs created, but by churches strengthened, people discipled, mercy shown, and institutions preserved faithfully.
- The Society's work should outlast founding leaders through strong documents, governance, prayer, and succession.

## Key Decisions

- The Society's vision is church-supporting, not church-replacing.
- The vision includes housing, work, mercy, leadership development, chaplaincy, and institutional stewardship.
