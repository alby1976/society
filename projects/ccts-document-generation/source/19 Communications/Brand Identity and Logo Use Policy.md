# Brand Identity and Logo Use Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs use of the Society's name, logo, visual identity, tagline, public descriptions, and brand assets.

Brand identity should support mission clarity, public trust, consistency, and proper authority.

---

# 2. Governing Principle

The Society's name and logo should not be used to claim authority, recognition, endorsement, or approval that has not been granted.

Brand use must follow governance.

---

# 3. Scope

This policy applies to:

- International Society;
- National Chapters;
- formation teams;
- ministries;
- committees;
- websites;
- social media;
- printed materials;
- donor materials;
- grant materials;
- event materials;
- presentations;
- public reports;
- signage;
- partner materials.

---

# 4. Protected Identity Elements

Identity elements may include:

- Society name;
- approved abbreviations;
- logo;
- icon;
- tagline;
- slogan;
- color palette;
- typography;
- public mission description;
- chapter naming pattern;
- ministry naming pattern.

---

# 5. Authority to Use Brand Assets

Brand assets may be used only by authorized persons or entities.

Authorization should define:

- asset;
- permitted use;
- duration;
- chapter or ministry;
- approval authority;
- restrictions;
- review date.

---

# 6. National Chapter Use

National Chapters may use Society identity according to recognition status.

| Status | Brand Use |
|---|---|
| Exploratory Region | No public brand use unless approved |
| Formation Team | Limited approved formation language |
| Provisional Chapter | Approved provisional chapter identity |
| Recognized Chapter | Approved national chapter identity |
| Restricted Chapter | Use limited by restrictions |
| Suspended Chapter | No public use unless specifically allowed |

---

# 7. Prohibited Uses

The Society's identity must not be used to:

- imply church status;
- imply endorsement by a church without permission;
- imply chapter recognition not granted;
- fundraise without authority;
- promise grants;
- admit residents;
- sign contracts;
- create unofficial ministries;
- support unrelated political or commercial activity;
- benefit private business without approval;
- misrepresent doctrine or mission.

---

# 8. Design Consistency

Brand materials should be consistent.

Consistency may include:

- correct logo;
- approved colors;
- readable layout;
- proper clear space;
- accurate name;
- approved tagline;
- proper chapter naming;
- accessible contrast;
- current contact information.

---

# 9. Local Adaptation

Local chapters may request adaptations for language, culture, accessibility, or legal context.

Adaptations should be recorded in the local adaptation register.

Translation or visual adaptation must not change mission, doctrine, or authority.

---

# 10. Partner Use

Partners, churches, contractors, or affiliated organizations may not use the Society's logo or name without permission.

Permission should specify:

- purpose;
- exact wording;
- where it will appear;
- duration;
- review rights;
- removal requirement.

---

# 11. Misuse

If brand identity is misused, the Society may require:

- correction;
- removal;
- public clarification;
- access removal;
- chapter restriction;
- legal review;
- corrective action;
- incident escalation.
