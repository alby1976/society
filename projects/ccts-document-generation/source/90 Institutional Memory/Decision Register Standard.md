# Decision Register Standard

## Purpose

To define how major decisions are recorded across the international Society and national chapters.

## Adopted Principles

- Decisions must be traceable.
- International, theological, board, chapter, housing, cooperative, and foundation decisions should use consistent prefixes.
- Decision records preserve institutional memory and reduce future confusion.

## Key Decisions

- Prefixes include INT, THEO, BOARD, CAN, HKG, HOUSE, COOP, FOUND, and GOV.
- Major decisions should identify reason, alternatives, approving body, related documents, and future review date.

## International Application

This document is written for an international society. National chapters may localize procedures required by civil law, but they may not alter the Society's theology, mission, reserved powers, or core governance identity without approval under the international governance framework.
