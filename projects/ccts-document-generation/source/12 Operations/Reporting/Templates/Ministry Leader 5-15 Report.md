# Ministry Leader 5-15 Report

## Template Instructions

This template is for ministry leaders responsible for coordinating a Society ministry, program, team, or recurring activity.

The report should help supervisors and executives understand ministry progress, people served, volunteer health, risks, church relationships, mission alignment, and decisions needed.

Do not include unnecessary confidential personal details. Use the proper confidentiality level and escalate urgent concerns through the appropriate channel.
