# 2. Confidential Information

I understand that I may receive or access confidential information through my role with the Society.

Confidential information may include:

- personal information;
- donor information;
- grant information;
- resident information;
- worker information;
- student support information;
- volunteer and staff information;
- church relationship information;
- chaplaincy or spiritual care information;
- financial information;
- legal information;
- safeguarding or incident information;
- board confidential information;
- records not approved for public release.

---

# 3. Confidentiality Commitment

I agree to:

- protect confidential information;
- use confidential information only for my approved role;
- share confidential information only with authorized persons who need to know;
- avoid gossip or unnecessary disclosure;
- store records only in approved locations;
- report suspected privacy or confidentiality breaches promptly;
- return or delete records as instructed when my role ends;
- continue confidentiality after my role ends.

---

# 4. Limits of Confidentiality

I understand that confidentiality does not prevent proper reporting of:

- safety concerns;
- safeguarding concerns;
- legal requirements;
- privacy breaches;
- financial misconduct;
- serious incidents;
- required governance reporting.

I will not promise absolute secrecy where the Society or law may require escalation.

---

# 5. Records and Devices

I agree to follow Society policy for:

- email;
- shared drives;
- paper records;
- personal devices;
- passwords;
- downloads;
- printing;
- forwarding;
- archiving;
- deletion.

---

# 6. Breach Reporting

If I believe confidential information has been lost, shared improperly, accessed improperly, or exposed, I will promptly report it to the appropriate Society contact.
