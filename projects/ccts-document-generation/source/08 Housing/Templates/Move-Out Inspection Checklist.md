# Move-Out Inspection Checklist

## Template Instructions

This checklist is used when a resident exits Society-related housing.

Use this checklist according to local law, lease terms, privacy rules, and the [[Housing Exit and Transition Policy]]. Attach photos only where lawful and necessary. Do not use the checklist to shame residents. Use it to document the condition of the property fairly.
