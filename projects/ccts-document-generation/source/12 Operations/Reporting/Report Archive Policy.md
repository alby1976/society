# Report Archive Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes how reports are archived within the Christian Community Transformation Society.

Reports include 5-15 reports, monthly summaries, quarterly reports, annual reports, board reports, committee reports, national chapter reports, ministry reports, mission alignment reports, and affiliated organization reports.

The archive preserves memory while protecting confidentiality and avoiding unnecessary accumulation.

---

# 2. Governing Principle

Reports should be archived because they serve memory, accountability, review, prayer, and continuity.

The archive is not a junk drawer. It is a structured memory system.

If reports are thrown into a folder called "misc old stuff," the folder has already won.

---

# 3. Archive Categories

Reports should be archived under categories such as:

- Governance;
- Board;
- Committees;
- National Chapters;
- Ministries;
- Housing;
- Worker Cooperative;
- Church Support Foundation;
- Chaplaincy;
- Finance;
- Risk;
- Mission Alignment;
- Theology;
- Church Partnerships;
- Annual Reports;
- 5-15 Reports;
- Confidential Reports.

---

# 4. Archive Levels

Reports should be assigned an archive level.

| Archive Level | Description |
|---|---|
| Operational | Routine reports retained for a defined period |
| Leadership | Reports useful for leadership continuity |
| Governance | Reports supporting board oversight and decisions |
| Historical | Reports preserved for institutional history |
| Permanent | Reports retained permanently |
| Restricted | Reports requiring special access controls |

A report may have more than one characteristic, such as Governance and Restricted.

---

# 5. Folder Structure

Recommended archive structure:

```text
source/
  90 Institutional Memory/
    Reports Archive/
      2026/
        5-15/
        Monthly/
        Quarterly/
        Annual/
        Governance/
        Restricted/
```

For large archives:

```text
Reports Archive/
  Permanent/
  Restricted/
  Operational/
  National Chapters/
  Ministries/
  Annual Reports/
```

The structure may be adjusted as the generator develops.

---

# 6. Naming Standard

Recommended report naming:

```text
YYYY-MM-DD - Report Type - Role or Ministry - Short Title.md
```

Examples:

```text
2026-06-28 - 5-15 - Chaplain - Weekly Report.md
2026-06-30 - Monthly - Housing - Ministry Summary.md
2026-Q2 - Quarterly - Canada Chapter - Board Summary.md
2026 - Annual - International Society - Ministry Report.md
```

Names should be boring and searchable. Creative file names are where archives go to get lost.

---

# 7. Archive Metadata

Archived reports should include metadata such as:

```yaml
report-type:
reporting-period:
role:
ministry:
chapter:
confidentiality:
archive-level:
retention-period:
permanent-preservation:
legal-hold:
submitted-by:
reviewed-by:
related-documents:
tags:
```

The generator may use this metadata to create indexes, dashboards, retention schedules, and review lists.

---

# 8. Permanent Archive

The permanent archive should include:

- annual reports;
- quarterly board summaries;
- mission alignment reviews;
- major governance reports;
- reports tied to constitutional changes;
- national chapter launch reports;
- major crisis reports;
- theological review reports;
- major property or housing reports;
- major foundation grant reports;
- worker cooperative launch reports;
- chaplaincy program milestone reports;
- founder transition reports;
- stewardship advisor reports.

---

# 9. Restricted Archive

Restricted archives should contain reports involving:

- pastoral care;
- chaplaincy sensitive matters;
- safeguarding;
- legal disputes;
- personnel;
- confidential financial matters;
- housing residents;
- grant applicants;
- member discipline;
- privacy matters;
- national chapter disputes.

Restricted archives require access controls.

---

# 10. Operational Archive

Operational archives contain routine reports retained for a defined period.

Operational reports may later be deleted according to [[5-15 Report Retention Policy]] and records policy, unless they meet permanent preservation criteria.

---

# 11. Monthly and Annual Summaries

Routine reports should feed summaries.

The archive should preserve:

- monthly summaries for leadership continuity;
- quarterly summaries for governance;
- annual summaries permanently.

This allows the Society to reduce raw report volume while preserving historical meaning.

---

# 12. Archive Review

The Society should review the archive annually.

The review should identify:

- reports due for deletion;
- reports requiring permanent preservation;
- missing annual summaries;
- reports with incorrect confidentiality;
- legal holds;
- orphaned files;
- duplicate records;
- broken links;
- incomplete metadata.

---

# 13. Deletion from Archive

Deletion must follow retention policy.

Before deletion, the Society should verify:

- retention period expired;
- no legal hold exists;
- no permanent preservation criterion applies;
- summary has been retained if needed;
- approval authority is documented.

Deletion should be recorded.

---

# 14. National Chapter Archives

National chapters should maintain report archives consistent with this policy and local law.

Internationally significant reports should be copied or summarized into the International Society archive.

---

# 15. Generator Integration

The generator should eventually produce:

- report index;
- archive index;
- reports by year;
- reports by ministry;
- reports by chapter;
- reports by confidentiality level;
- reports approaching deletion;
- permanent archive list;
- missing metadata report.
