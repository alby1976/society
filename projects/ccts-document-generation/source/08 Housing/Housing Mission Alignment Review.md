# Housing Mission Alignment Review

## 1. Purpose

This document provides a housing-specific Mission Alignment Review process for Society-related housing ministries.

Housing ministry is powerful because it touches home, stability, money, property, community, privacy, and spiritual care. It must therefore be reviewed regularly to ensure it remains faithful to the Society's mission and does not drift into mere real estate management, coercive community control, financial irresponsibility, or unhealthy dependency.

This document applies the general [[Mission Alignment Review]] system to housing.

---

# 2. Governing Principle

Housing must serve the Society's mission. The mission must not become a servant of housing.

If a housing ministry no longer protects resident dignity, supports church-connected Christian community, preserves lawful stewardship, and remains financially sustainable, it requires correction.

---

# 3. Review Frequency

Housing Mission Alignment Review should occur:

- annually during launch or growth;
- at least every three years once stable;
- before major property acquisition;
- before major property disposal;
- before launching a new housing program;
- after serious housing incidents;
- after recurring complaints;
- after significant financial concern;
- when mission drift is suspected.

---

# 4. Review Authority

The review may be conducted by:

- housing committee;
- National Board;
- International Board delegate;
- Mission Alignment Review team;
- external reviewer;
- Church Advisory representative where church-related issues are involved;
- Theological Oversight representative where doctrinal issues are involved.

The reviewer should not have an unmanaged conflict of interest.

---

# 5. Review Materials

The reviewer should examine:

- [[Housing Trust Framework]];
- [[Housing Governance Policy]];
- [[Tenant and Resident Policy]];
- [[Lease Standards]];
- [[Community Expectations Policy]];
- [[Stewardship Housing Policy]];
- [[Housing Affordability Policy]];
- [[Housing Risk Register]];
- [[Annual Housing Report]];
- resident support summaries;
- complaint summaries;
- incident summaries;
- financial reports;
- maintenance and reserve reports;
- lease or agreement samples;
- church referral summary;
- chaplaincy summary;
- board minutes or decisions.

Sensitive resident information should be minimized.

---

# 6. Review Questions

## 6.1 Mission

- Does the housing ministry clearly serve the Society's mission?
- Who is being served?
- Why is housing the right tool?
- Does the ministry still support local churches?
- Has property management displaced spiritual and mercy purpose?
- Are residents treated as neighbors rather than projects?

## 6.2 Christian Identity

- Is the Society's Christian identity clear?
- Are prayer, chaplaincy, and church referral offered appropriately?
- Is religious participation voluntary unless lawfully and clearly required?
- Is anyone pressured to perform belief to receive housing?
- Is the Society/church distinction preserved?

## 6.3 Resident Dignity

- Are residents treated fairly?
- Are privacy and confidentiality protected?
- Are support needs handled respectfully?
- Are complaints heard?
- Are exits handled with dignity?
- Are vulnerable residents protected from exploitation?

## 6.4 Legal and Governance

- Are lease forms lawful and reviewed?
- Are eligibility criteria clear and lawful?
- Are conflicts of interest disclosed?
- Are decisions documented?
- Are reserved powers respected?
- Are reports submitted?
- Are policies actually used?

## 6.5 Financial Stewardship

- Is the housing ministry financially sustainable?
- Are reserves adequate?
- Are subsidies documented?
- Are affordability standards clear?
- Is debt manageable?
- Is maintenance being deferred dangerously?
- Are donor restrictions properly honored?

## 6.6 Community Life

- Are community expectations clear and lawful?
- Are residents safe?
- Are conflicts addressed?
- Are shared spaces managed?
- Are resident relationships generally healthy?
- Are staff or volunteers overreaching?

## 6.7 Risk

- Are risks tracked in [[Housing Risk Register]]?
- Are high risks escalated?
- Are incidents reviewed?
- Are safety concerns addressed?
- Are privacy concerns managed?
- Are mission drift warning signs present?

---

# 7. Rating Scale

Each area may be rated as follows:

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Strong | Clearly aligned and healthy |
| Stable | Generally aligned with ordinary improvements needed |
| Watch | Concerns exist and require monitoring |
| Corrective Action Required | Significant concerns require action |
| Critical | Serious mission, legal, financial, safety, or governance concern |

---

# 8. Review Findings

The review should produce findings in these categories:

1. strengths;
2. concerns;
3. risks;
4. required corrections;
5. recommended improvements;
6. decisions needed;
7. policy revisions needed;
8. follow-up date;
9. responsible persons.

---

# 9. Corrective Action

Corrective actions may include:

- policy revision;
- resident support improvement;
- lease review;
- legal review;
- financial review;
- reserve plan;
- church referral improvement;
- chaplaincy clarification;
- staff training;
- complaint process correction;
- governance restructuring;
- property disposal review;
- pause on expansion;
- board intervention.

A review without follow-up is just paperwork with a nicer title.

---

# 10. Reporting

Housing Mission Alignment Review findings should be reported to the appropriate board and summarized in the [[Annual Housing Report]].

Confidential resident details should not be included unless necessary and properly restricted.
