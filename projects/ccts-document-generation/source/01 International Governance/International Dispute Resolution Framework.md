# International Dispute Resolution Framework

## Purpose

To provide a fair, biblical, and orderly process for resolving disputes between international bodies, national chapters, leaders, members, and ministries.

## Adopted Principles

- Disputes should be handled with truth, charity, patience, due process, and church-sensitive wisdom.
- Spiritual concerns involving church members should not bypass lawful local church leadership.
- Governance disputes must be documented and reviewable.

## Key Decisions

- Escalation should move from local resolution to national review to international review.
- Theological disputes may require review by the International Theological Oversight Council.

## International Application

This document is written for an international society. National chapters may localize procedures required by civil law, but they may not alter the Society's theology, mission, reserved powers, or core governance identity without approval under the international governance framework.
