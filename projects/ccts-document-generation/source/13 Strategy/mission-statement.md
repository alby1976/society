# Mission Statement

## Mission

To advance the Christian religion by supporting faithful local churches and developing ministries that demonstrate the Gospel through discipleship, mercy, meaningful work, affordable housing, leadership development, and community transformation.

## Purpose

The Society exists to preserve and extend a confessionally Reformed, church-supporting ministry framework that connects worship, work, mercy, prayer, housing, stewardship, and community service.

## Adopted Principles

- Mission must remain explicitly Christian and confessionally Reformed.
- Ministries must be accountable to the Society's theological and governance boundaries.
- The Society may create or oversee supporting organizations that help fulfill its purpose, vision, and mission.
- Mission preservation requires governance documents, advisory structures, review processes, and institutional memory.

## Key Decisions

- The Society complements churches rather than replacing them.
- All supporting organizations remain under Society oversight.
- Ministry initiatives must be reviewed for mission alignment.
