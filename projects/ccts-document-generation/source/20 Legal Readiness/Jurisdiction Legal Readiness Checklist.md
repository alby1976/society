# Jurisdiction Legal Readiness Checklist

## 1. Purpose

This checklist helps the Society evaluate legal readiness in any country, province, state, region, or other jurisdiction before formation, recognition, fundraising, hiring, housing, grants, cooperative activity, or public launch.

This checklist does not replace local legal advice.
