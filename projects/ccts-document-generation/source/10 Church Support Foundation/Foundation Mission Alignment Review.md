# Foundation Mission Alignment Review

## 1. Purpose

This document provides a Foundation-specific Mission Alignment Review process for the Church Support Foundation of the Christian Community Transformation Society.

The review exists to ensure that Foundation funds, grants, scholarships, donor restrictions, recipient relationships, and reporting practices remain faithful to the Society's mission, theological identity, church-supporting purpose, and governance standards.

This document applies the general [[Mission Alignment Review]] system to Foundation work.

---

# 2. Governing Principle

The Foundation must support ministry without controlling the church, enriching insiders, weakening accountability, or allowing donor preferences to replace mission.

Grant-making is not neutral administration. It shapes priorities, incentives, relationships, dependencies, and institutional memory.

---

# 3. Review Frequency

Foundation Mission Alignment Review should occur:

- annually during Foundation launch;
- at least every three years once stable;
- before launching a major new grant category;
- before accepting a major restricted gift;
- before approving a high-risk grant;
- after serious grant misuse;
- after recurring reporting failures;
- after donor influence concerns;
- when church relationship concerns arise;
- when theological concerns arise;
- when mission drift is suspected.

---

# 4. Review Authority

The review may be conducted by:

- Foundation board or committee;
- National Board;
- International Board delegate;
- Mission Alignment Review team;
- external reviewer;
- Theological Oversight representative where doctrinal issues arise;
- Church Advisory representative where church relationship issues arise.

The reviewer should not have an unmanaged conflict of interest.

# 6. Review Questions

## 6.1 Mission

- Do Foundation grants clearly support the Society's mission?
- Are grants strengthening faithful ministry rather than distracting from it?
- Are grants aligned with approved grant categories?
- Are grants creating unhealthy dependency?
- Are grant decisions documented and understandable?
- Is the Foundation drifting into general philanthropy without clear mission?

## 6.2 Doctrine and Church-Supporting Identity

- Are grants consistent with the Society's Statement of Faith?
- Are theological education grants reviewed properly?
- Are church relationships respected?
- Is the Foundation avoiding control over preaching, worship, sacraments, discipline, and church governance?
- Are church advisory concerns being heard?
- Are doctrinal concerns escalated to Theological Oversight where needed?

## 6.3 Stewardship

- Are funds used for approved purposes?
- Are grant agreements clear?
- Are financial records complete?
- Are restricted funds tracked?
- Are unused funds handled properly?
- Are reporting requirements proportionate?
- Are grants reviewed before renewal?

## 6.4 Donor Restrictions

- Are donor restrictions mission-aligned?
- Are restrictions lawful and administratively feasible?
- Are donors improperly influencing grant decisions?
- Are restricted funds tracked separately?
- Are donor reports truthful and appropriately limited?
- Are donor preferences being allowed to distort the Foundation's priorities?

## 6.5 Recipient Accountability

- Are recipients reporting?
- Are overdue reports followed up?
- Are changes to grant purpose approved?
- Are grants closed properly?
- Are recurring grants reviewed before renewal?
- Are recipients treated with dignity rather than suspicion?

## 6.6 Conflicts of Interest

- Are conflicts disclosed?
- Are conflicted people excluded from decisions where required?
- Are related-party grants documented?
- Are churches, students, vendors, or ministries connected to reviewers handled fairly?
- Are insider benefits prevented?

## 6.7 Risk

- Are risks tracked in [[Foundation Risk Register]]?
- Are high risks escalated?
- Are legal, financial, theological, donor, or church risks reviewed?
- Are privacy risks controlled?
- Are grant misuse concerns handled appropriately?

---

# 7. Rating Scale

Each area may be rated as follows:

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Strong | Clearly aligned and healthy |
| Stable | Generally aligned with ordinary improvements needed |
| Watch | Concerns exist and require monitoring |
| Corrective Action Required | Significant concerns require action |
| Critical | Serious mission, legal, financial, donor, doctrinal, church, or governance concern |

---

# 8. Review Findings

The review should produce findings in these categories:

1. strengths;
2. concerns;
3. risks;
4. required corrections;
5. recommended improvements;
6. decisions needed;
7. policy revisions needed;
8. follow-up date;
9. responsible persons.

---

# 9. Corrective Action

Corrective actions may include:

- grant policy revision;
- restricted gift review;
- donor communication correction;
- grant suspension;
- recipient follow-up;
- reporting simplification;
- stronger financial controls;
- conflict of interest remedy;
- theological review;
- church advisory review;
- board review;
- legal review;
- pause on grant category;
- closure of a grant program.

A Foundation review without follow-up becomes an expensive way to admire problems.
