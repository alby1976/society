# General 5-15 Report

## Template Instructions

This is the Society's general 5-15 Report template.

A 5-15 Report should take approximately fifteen minutes to prepare and approximately five minutes to read. It should be concise, honest, useful, and prayer-aware.

Do not include unnecessary confidential details. Use the correct confidentiality level. Escalate urgent concerns through the proper channel rather than burying them inside a routine report.
