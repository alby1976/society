# National Chapter Recognition Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes how the Christian Community Transformation Society recognizes, reviews, suspends, or revokes National Chapters.

Recognition is a serious act. It authorizes a local national body to represent the Society's mission, doctrine, identity, governance, and public trust within a jurisdiction.

---

# 2. Governing Principle

Recognition shall be earned by readiness, not assumed by enthusiasm.

A group may sincerely desire to serve the mission, but a National Chapter also requires doctrine, governance, law, reporting, leadership, finances, accountability, and church-supporting clarity.

---

# 3. Recognition Categories

The International Board may recognize the following categories.

## 3.1 Exploratory Region

A country or region being assessed.

## 3.2 Formation Team

A group authorized to explore legal, church, and governance readiness.

## 3.3 Provisional National Chapter

A chapter authorized to organize under conditions but not yet fully recognized.

## 3.4 Recognized National Chapter

A chapter formally recognized to conduct Society work under defined authority.

## 3.5 Restricted National Chapter

A chapter under corrective limitation.

## 3.6 Suspended National Chapter

A chapter whose authority to operate under Society identity is paused.

## 3.7 Derecognized Chapter

A chapter whose recognition has been revoked.

---

# 4. Recognition Authority

Only the International Board may grant full recognition to a National Chapter unless the International Constitution later delegates a defined portion of this authority.

Recognition decisions should be recorded in the Decision Register.

---

# 5. Minimum Recognition Requirements

Before recognition, a chapter should demonstrate:

- acceptance of the Society's Statement of Faith;
- acceptance of the Society's mission, vision, and values;
- willingness to operate under the International Constitution;
- acceptance of reserved powers;
- lawful local structure or credible legal pathway;
- qualified founding leadership;
- financial controls;
- conflict of interest policy;
- reporting plan;
- church advisory plan;
- risk review;
- ministry launch plan;
- document registry;
- decision register;
- Mission Alignment Review readiness.

---

# 6. Application for Recognition

An application should include:

- proposed chapter name;
- country or jurisdiction;
- legal form;
- founding leadership;
- church relationships;
- ministry plan;
- budget;
- risk assessment;
- governance documents;
- legal review summary;
- reporting plan;
- mission alignment statement;
- requested authority;
- local adaptation requests.

---

# 7. Review Process

Recognition review may include:

1. application intake;
2. doctrine and mission review;
3. legal review;
4. leadership review;
5. church advisory review;
6. financial review;
7. risk review;
8. reporting readiness review;
9. local adaptation review;
10. International Board decision;
11. written recognition letter;
12. recognition conditions;
13. review schedule.

---

# 8. Provisional Recognition

Provisional recognition may be granted when a chapter is promising but not yet fully ready.

Provisional recognition should define:

- term;
- permitted activities;
- prohibited activities;
- reporting requirements;
- legal steps required;
- leadership requirements;
- financial limits;
- ministry limits;
- conditions for full recognition;
- review date.

Provisional recognition should not become permanent limbo.

---

# 9. Recognition Conditions

Recognition may include conditions such as:

- adopting required policies;
- completing legal registration;
- appointing qualified leaders;
- forming advisory relationships;
- submitting reports;
- completing financial controls;
- limiting ministries during launch;
- obtaining insurance;
- completing Mission Alignment Review;
- resolving conflicts of interest.

---

# 10. Recognition Letter

A recognition letter should identify:

- chapter name;
- jurisdiction;
- recognition status;
- effective date;
- authority granted;
- restrictions;
- reserved powers;
- reporting duties;
- review date;
- signatures;
- related decision record.

---

# 11. Annual Confirmation

Recognized chapters should provide annual confirmation of:

- doctrine;
- mission;
- legal standing;
- board membership;
- financial controls;
- reporting;
- ministries;
- risks;
- church relationships;
- key changes;
- compliance with reserved powers.

---

# 12. Restriction

A chapter may be restricted if concerns arise.

Restrictions may include:

- increased reporting;
- pause on new ministries;
- spending limits;
- grant limits;
- leadership review;
- prohibition on public claims;
- pause on hiring;
- pause on property acquisition;
- requirement for external review.

---

# 13. Suspension

A chapter may be suspended for serious concerns such as:

- doctrinal departure;
- mission drift;
- governance failure;
- legal noncompliance;
- financial misconduct;
- unresolved conflict of interest;
- failure to report;
- misuse of Society identity;
- safeguarding concern;
- refusal of accountability.

Suspension should be documented and communicated carefully.

---

# 14. Derecognition

Derecognition may occur when restoration is not possible or appropriate.

Derecognition should address:

- use of name;
- documents;
- assets;
- donor communications;
- legal obligations;
- public communications;
- records;
- ministry transition;
- resident, worker, or grant recipient impact where applicable.

---

# 15. Appeals or Reconsideration

A chapter may request reconsideration where allowed by International Board policy.

Reconsideration may be appropriate when:

- new information is available;
- process was not followed;
- corrective action has been completed;
- misunderstanding occurred;
- legal status changed.

---

# 16. Records

Recognition records should be retained permanently.

Records include:

- application;
- reviews;
- conflicts;
- legal documents;
- Board decision;
- recognition letter;
- conditions;
- annual confirmations;
- corrective actions;
- suspension or derecognition records.

---

# 17. Review

This policy should be reviewed annually during international formation and whenever a chapter is recognized, suspended, or derecognized.
