# Theological Foundations

## Executive Summary

The Christian Community Transformation Society exists under the Lordship of Jesus Christ to serve, support, and strengthen faithful local churches. The Society is not a church, does not replace the church, and does not assume the offices or ordinances entrusted to the church. Its work is a supporting ministry of Christian mercy, discipleship, stewardship, and community transformation.

## Adopted Principles

- Scripture is the supreme authority for faith, life, doctrine, and ministry.
- Jesus Christ is Head of the Church and Lord over every part of life.
- The Society serves churches rather than competing with them.
- Lord's Day worship is not replaced by weekday ministry; rather, Lord's Day worship shapes and transforms the rest of the week.
- Christian charity includes both spiritual and practical mercy.
- All ministries must remain confessionally Reformed.
- Prayer accompanies every significant decision, stage, and ministry development.

## Key Decisions

- The Society shall not become a church.
- The Society shall not administer sacraments.
- The Society shall not exercise church discipline.
- The Society shall encourage people to participate in faithful local churches.
- The Society shall preserve theological identity through governance, advisory, and review structures.
