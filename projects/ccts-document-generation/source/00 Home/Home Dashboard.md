> Equipping Christ's Church to transform communities through faithful worship, compassionate service, meaningful work, affordable housing, leadership development, and biblical stewardship under the Lordship of Jesus Christ.

---

# Welcome

Welcome to the Living Governance Vault of the Christian Community Transformation Society.

This vault is the Society's source of truth for theology, mission, governance, international expansion, institutional memory, policies, reporting, and ministry frameworks.

The Society is designed as an international Christian society from the beginning. Canada may serve as the founding national chapter, but the mission, theology, and governance architecture are international in scope.
