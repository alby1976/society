# Theological Oversight Council

## Executive Summary

The Theological Oversight Council provides theological review and counsel to help preserve the Society's doctrinal identity and mission fidelity.

## Adopted Principles

- The Council protects doctrine, mission, and theological identity.
- The Council advises; the Board governs.
- The Council should review major ministry frameworks, theological disputes, doctrinal questions, chaplaincy qualifications, and mission alignment concerns.

## Key Decisions

- The Council shall not replace church courts or elders.
- Its authority is advisory unless specific governing documents assign a reserved review function.
- Its work should be integrated with the Mission Alignment Review system.
