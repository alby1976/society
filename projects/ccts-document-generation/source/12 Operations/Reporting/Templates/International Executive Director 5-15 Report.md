# International Executive Director 5-15 Report

## Template Instructions

This template is for the International Executive Director or equivalent senior executive responsible for international coordination and implementation.

The report should help the International Board understand global mission alignment, national chapter health, major risks, international partnerships, governance issues, resource needs, decisions required, and prayer needs.

Use the proper confidentiality level. Do not include unnecessary personal, legal, pastoral, employment, or sensitive chapter details.
