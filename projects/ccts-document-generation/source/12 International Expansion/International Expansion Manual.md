# International Expansion Manual

## Purpose

To guide prayerful, legally careful, church-sensitive expansion into new countries.

## Adopted Principles

- Expansion begins with prayer.
- International theology and mission come first.
- Local legal research is required.
- Church relationships come before ministry launch.
- No chapter should be recognized before governance readiness is demonstrated.

## Key Decisions

- Expansion path: prayer, mission assessment, church relationships, legal research, national advisory team, chapter formation, recognition, launch, annual review.

## International Application

This document is written for an international society. National chapters may localize procedures required by civil law, but they may not alter the Society's theology, mission, reserved powers, or core governance identity without approval under the international governance framework.
