# Membership Covenant

## 1. Purpose

This Membership Covenant describes the commitments expected of members of the Christian Community Transformation Society.

The covenant is not a church covenant and does not replace membership in a faithful local church. It is an organizational covenant of mission alignment, conduct, stewardship, and accountability.

This document should be read with [[Membership Framework]], [[Membership Categories]], [[Statement of Faith]], [[Mission Statement]], and [[Core Values]].

---

# 2. Covenant Identity

As a member of the Society, I understand that the Society exists to advance the Christian religion by supporting faithful local churches and developing ministries that demonstrate the Gospel through discipleship, mercy, meaningful work, affordable housing, leadership development, and community transformation under the Lordship of Jesus Christ.

I understand that the Society is not a church and does not replace the local church.

---

# 3. Doctrinal Commitment

According to my membership category, I affirm, receive, or respectfully acknowledge the Society's [[Statement of Faith]] and agree not to oppose the Society's theological identity.

For voting, governing, teaching, chaplaincy, or theological roles, fuller doctrinal affirmation may be required by policy.

---

# 4. Mission Commitment

I will support the Society's mission, vision, and core values.

I will not use membership to redirect the Society toward a different mission, political agenda, personal project, financial interest, or theological identity.

---

# 5. Church-Supporting Commitment

I understand that the Society exists to support faithful local churches.

Where reasonably possible, I will participate in the life of a faithful local church and encourage others to do the same.

I will not treat the Society as a replacement for Lord's Day worship, church membership, pastoral oversight, sacraments, or church discipline.

---

# 6. Conduct Commitment

I will seek to conduct myself with honesty, humility, patience, integrity, and love.

I will avoid conduct that knowingly harms the Society's mission, witness, members, beneficiaries, churches, staff, volunteers, residents, workers, or partners.

I understand that serious misconduct may result in discipline, suspension, or removal according to policy.

---

# 7. Stewardship Commitment

I will respect the Society's resources as gifts entrusted by God.

This includes:

- funds;
- property;
- documents;
- reports;
- confidential information;
- volunteer time;
- staff capacity;
- relationships;
- institutional memory;
- reputation.

I will not misuse Society resources for private benefit.

---

# 8. Accountability Commitment

I will respect the Society's governing documents and lawful authorities.

I will submit concerns through appropriate channels and avoid factionalism, manipulation, or hidden campaigns that damage trust.

I understand that accountability is part of faithful stewardship.

---

# 9. Conflict of Interest Commitment

I will disclose actual, potential, or perceived conflicts of interest when they arise.

I will not participate improperly in decisions where my personal interests, relationships, finances, or outside obligations may compromise judgment.

---

# 10. Confidentiality Commitment

I will protect confidential information entrusted to me.

Confidential information may include personal, pastoral, financial, legal, employment, housing, grant, donor, board, or ministry information.

I understand that confidentiality must not be used to hide wrongdoing or prevent proper reporting.

---

# 11. Prayer Commitment

I will pray for the Society as I am able.

I will pray for:

- faithfulness to Christ;
- local churches;
- board and leaders;
- national chapters;
- workers and volunteers;
- residents and beneficiaries;
- chaplains;
- housing ministry;
- worker cooperative ministry;
- church support foundation ministry;
- wisdom and protection from mission drift.

---

# 12. Service Commitment

Where I am able and called, I will support the Society through service, encouragement, giving, prayer, counsel, or participation appropriate to my membership category.

Membership should not be reduced to name-on-a-list Christianity. The clipboard may know your name, but the mission needs your heart.

---

# 13. Reporting Commitment

If my role requires reporting, I will submit reports honestly and on time.

Reports may include:

- 5-15 reports;
- ministry updates;
- volunteer reports;
- committee reports;
- conflict disclosures;
- incident reports;
- annual affirmations.

Reporting is part of accountability and institutional memory.

---

# 14. Unity Commitment

I will pursue peace and unity consistent with truth.

I will not confuse unity with silence about serious concerns.

I will seek to address disagreements honestly, charitably, and through proper channels.

---

# 15. International Commitment

I understand that the Society is international by design.

I will respect the shared doctrine, mission, and governance standards of the international Society while recognizing that national chapters may lawfully adapt procedures to local contexts.

---

# 16. Membership Acknowledgment

A member may be asked to affirm:

> I have read and understood the Membership Covenant. I support the mission of the Christian Community Transformation Society. I understand that the Society is not a church and exists to support faithful local churches. I agree to conduct myself consistently with this covenant and the Society's governing documents according to my membership category.

---

# 17. Review

This covenant should be reviewed annually and whenever the Society revises membership categories, membership discipline, or doctrinal requirements.
