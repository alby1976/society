# Chaplaincy Framework

## Purpose

To define chaplaincy as a ministry of the Society that supports spiritual care without replacing pastors, elders, or local churches.

## Adopted Principles

- The chaplain serves the Society, not merely the worker cooperative.
- The chaplain must not contradict local church leadership.
- The chaplain may care temporarily for the unchurched while directing them toward faithful local churches.
- The chaplain should have theological training from a seminary recognized by the Church Advisory Fellowship.

## Key Decisions

- The chaplain may facilitate the Annual Day of Prayer and give a devotional meditation.
- Chaplaincy standards should eventually scale to Chief Chaplain, National Chaplains, and Regional Chaplains.

## International Application

This document is written for an international society. National chapters may localize procedures required by civil law, but they may not alter the Society's theology, mission, reserved powers, or core governance identity without approval under the international governance framework.
