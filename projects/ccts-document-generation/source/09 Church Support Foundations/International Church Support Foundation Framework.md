# International Church Support Foundation Framework

## Purpose

To define the foundation model for supporting churches, ministry leaders, students, capacity-building grants, and long-term stewardship.

## Adopted Principles

- The foundation exists to strengthen faithful churches and ministries.
- Grants should build capacity rather than create dependency.
- Student support should be connected to genuine ministry purpose and accountability.
- Foundation development should follow governance and ministry maturity.

## Key Decisions

- Capacity-building grants are preferred.
- Recovery or repayment terms may be considered where a funded student abandons the agreed ministry path.
- The foundation should come after the pilot cooperative and stronger governance base.

## International Application

This document is written for an international society. National chapters may localize procedures required by civil law, but they may not alter the Society's theology, mission, reserved powers, or core governance identity without approval under the international governance framework.
