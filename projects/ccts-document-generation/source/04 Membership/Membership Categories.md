# Membership Categories

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the membership categories of the Christian Community Transformation Society.

Membership categories clarify who participates in the Society, what rights and responsibilities they have, what doctrinal or mission alignment is required, and how membership relates to international and national governance.

This policy should be read with [[Membership Framework]], [[Membership Covenant]], and [[Member Discipline Policy]].

---

# 2. Governing Principle

Membership categories should support mission and accountability without creating confusion about church membership, board authority, or national chapter governance.

Different people may relate to the Society in different ways. A donor, volunteer, board member, church partner, and voting member should not all be treated as though they hold the same authority.

---

# 3. General Membership Standards

All membership categories require:

- respect for the Society's mission;
- willingness to be governed by applicable Society documents;
- truthful application information;
- conduct consistent with the Society's witness;
- compliance with applicable policies;
- respect for local churches;
- no intentional effort to undermine the Society's doctrine.

Additional requirements vary by category.

---

# 4. Founding Members

## 4.1 Definition

Founding Members are those recognized as having participated in the establishment, launch, or early governance of the Society.

## 4.2 Purpose

Founding membership recognizes historical contribution and preserves institutional memory.

## 4.3 Rights

Founding Members may receive:

- historical recognition;
- invitation to major anniversary events;
- opportunity to provide counsel;
- eligibility for stewardship advisor consideration;
- access to appropriate public reports.

Voting rights depend on whether the Founding Member also qualifies under another voting membership class.

## 4.4 Limits

Founding status does not create permanent control, veto power, ownership, or immunity from policy.

The Society should honor founders without becoming trapped by founder dependency.

---

# 5. Voting Members

## 5.1 Definition

Voting Members are members authorized by bylaws or policy to vote on defined Society matters.

## 5.2 Eligibility

Voting Members should normally:

- affirm the Society's [[Statement of Faith]] at the required level;
- support the [[Mission Statement]], [[Vision Statement]], and [[Core Values]];
- be members in good standing of faithful local churches where reasonably possible;
- complete membership orientation;
- agree to the [[Membership Covenant]];
- meet any national chapter legal requirements.

## 5.3 Rights

Voting Members may vote on matters assigned to members by bylaws, law, or policy.

## 5.4 Responsibilities

Voting Members should:

- pray for the Society;
- participate responsibly;
- review materials before voting;
- avoid factionalism;
- disclose conflicts;
- vote according to mission rather than personal interest.

## 5.5 Limits

Voting Members may not override reserved powers, alter doctrine outside constitutional process, direct staff outside proper authority, or act as a shadow board.

---

# 6. Supporting Members

## 6.1 Definition

Supporting Members are persons who support the Society's mission through prayer, encouragement, financial giving, volunteer service, advocacy, or other assistance without holding voting rights.

## 6.2 Eligibility

Supporting Members should:

- affirm or respect the Society's mission;
- agree not to oppose the Society's theological identity;
- comply with conduct expectations;
- complete any required application or registration.

## 6.3 Rights

Supporting Members may receive updates, participate in appropriate events, volunteer where approved, and be invited to consultation.

## 6.4 Limits

Supporting Members do not vote unless separately admitted to a voting class.

---

# 7. National Chapter Members

## 7.1 Definition

National Chapter Members are members associated with a recognized national chapter.

## 7.2 Local Structure

National chapters may define local member requirements consistent with international standards and local law.

## 7.3 International Alignment

National Chapter Members must not be organized in a way that undermines the International Society's doctrine, mission, or reserved powers.

---

# 8. Associate Members

## 8.1 Definition

Associate Members are persons connected to the Society for a specific purpose, limited term, project, training, or ministry relationship.

## 8.2 Use

Associate membership may be useful for:

- students;
- ministry trainees;
- short-term workers;
- project participants;
- advisory participants;
- supporters in countries without a national chapter.

## 8.3 Limits

Associate Members do not ordinarily vote or govern.

---

# 9. Church Partners

## 9.1 Definition

Church Partners are churches or church bodies that cooperate with the Society according to an approved church partnership policy.

## 9.2 Nature

A church partnership is not the same as individual membership.

Church Partners retain their own ecclesiastical authority. The Society does not govern churches.

## 9.3 Rights and Responsibilities

Church Partners may provide counsel, referrals, cooperation, prayer, volunteers, theological input, or local support according to the partnership agreement.

---

# 10. Honorary Members

## 10.1 Definition

Honorary Members are persons recognized for exceptional service, counsel, generosity, founding contribution, or mission support.

## 10.2 Limits

Honorary membership does not automatically carry voting rights, board eligibility, employment rights, or governance authority.

---

# 11. Volunteer Participants

## 11.1 Definition

Volunteer Participants are approved volunteers who serve in Society ministries but are not necessarily members.

## 11.2 Requirements

Volunteers may be required to:

- complete screening;
- complete training;
- follow conduct policies;
- submit reports;
- respect confidentiality;
- work under supervision.

## 11.3 Membership Distinction

Volunteer participation should not be confused with membership unless the person is formally admitted as a member.

---

# 12. Board Members

Board members may or may not be a separate membership class depending on bylaws and law.

All board members must meet [[Board Member Qualifications]] and any membership requirements required by governing documents.

---

# 13. Staff Members

Staff Members are employees or contractors of the Society or an affiliated organization.

Employment does not automatically create membership.

Staff may be members if admitted through the ordinary membership process and permitted by conflict of interest rules.

---

# 14. Chaplains

Chaplains serve under chaplaincy appointment, not merely membership.

A chaplain may also be a member, staff person, contractor, or volunteer depending on policy.

Chaplaincy requirements should be defined by [[Chaplain Policy]] and theological standards.

---

# 15. Membership Category Table

| Category | Voting | Typical Role | Doctrinal Requirement | Governance Authority |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Founding Member | Only if separately qualified | Historical recognition | According to class | No automatic authority |
| Voting Member | Yes | Formal member governance | Affirmation required | As bylaws allow |
| Supporting Member | No | Prayer, giving, service | Respect or affirmation as required | None |
| National Chapter Member | Maybe | Local participation | Local and international standards | As local bylaws allow |
| Associate Member | No | Limited participation | Mission respect | None |
| Church Partner | No individual vote unless defined | Church cooperation | Partnership standards | Advisory only |
| Honorary Member | No | Recognition | Case by case | None |
| Volunteer Participant | No | Service | Conduct and mission respect | None |
| Board Member | Board vote | Governance | Board standard | Board authority |
| Staff Member | No by employment alone | Operations | Role-specific | Delegated authority |

---

# 16. Transfers Between Categories

A member may move between categories if approved according to policy.

Transfers may require:

- application;
- review;
- doctrinal affirmation;
- board or officer approval;
- conflict disclosure;
- orientation.

---

# 17. Suspension of Category Rights

A member's rights may be suspended during review, discipline, conflict investigation, or loss of eligibility.

Suspension should be documented and subject to fair process.

---

# 18. Review

This policy should be reviewed annually and whenever bylaws, national chapter structures, or membership voting rules are changed.
