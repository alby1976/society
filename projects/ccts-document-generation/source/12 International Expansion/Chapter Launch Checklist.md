# Chapter Launch Checklist

## Purpose

To provide a practical launch checklist for new national chapters.

## Checklist

- [ ] Pray and seek counsel.
- [ ] Confirm alignment with [[Statement of Faith]].
- [ ] Confirm alignment with [[Mission Statement]] and [[Vision Statement]].
- [ ] Complete [[New Country Assessment Framework]].
- [ ] Identify local church relationships.
- [ ] Complete preliminary legal research.
- [ ] Identify founding advisory team.
- [ ] Draft national charter.
- [ ] Draft required national bylaws.
- [ ] Confirm financial controls.
- [ ] Confirm reporting structure.
- [ ] Submit recognition request.
- [ ] Receive international approval.
- [ ] Launch as recognized chapter.
- [ ] Schedule first Mission Alignment Review.

## Notes

This checklist should be localized by country but not weakened in theology, mission, or accountability.
