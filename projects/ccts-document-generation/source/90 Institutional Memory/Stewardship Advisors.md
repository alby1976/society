# Stewardship Advisors

## Purpose

To preserve institutional memory through former founders, former board members, and experienced leaders without creating shadow governance.

## Adopted Principles

- Former leaders may advise but do not govern unless formally appointed to office.
- Institutional memory should be documented, searchable, and passed on.
- Founder wisdom is valuable; founder control cannot be permanent.

## Key Decisions

- Stewardship Advisors may include former board members.
- Advisors support succession, orientation, history, and governance continuity.

## International Application

This document is written for an international society. National chapters may localize procedures required by civil law, but they may not alter the Society's theology, mission, reserved powers, or core governance identity without approval under the international governance framework.
