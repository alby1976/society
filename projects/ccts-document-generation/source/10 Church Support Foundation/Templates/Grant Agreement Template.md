# 7. Prohibited Uses

Grant funds may not be used for:

- expenses outside the approved purpose;
- unlawful activity;
- private benefit unrelated to grant purpose;
- political partisan activity;
- doctrinally contrary activity;
- undisclosed compensation;
- unapproved debt repayment;
- insider benefit;
- other prohibited uses listed in policy or approval conditions.

# 11. Misuse or Noncompliance

If funds are misused or reporting requirements are not met, the Foundation may:

- request clarification;
- pause further payments;
- require corrective action;
- require repayment where lawful and appropriate;
- decline future support;
- seek legal or board review;
- close the grant.

---

# 12. Recipient Commitments

The recipient agrees to:

- use funds only for approved purposes;
- maintain appropriate records;
- submit required reports;
- disclose material changes;
- disclose conflicts of interest;
- comply with applicable law;
- respect the Society's mission and grant conditions;
- return or redirect unused funds only as approved.

---

# 13. Foundation Commitments

The Foundation agrees to:

- disburse funds according to approval and payment schedule;
- protect confidential information;
- review reports fairly;
- communicate concerns in a timely manner;
- avoid improper control over local church authority.

---

# 14. Church and Theological Boundaries

Where the recipient is a church or church-connected ministry, this grant does not authorize the Foundation to control preaching, sacraments, discipline, membership, pastoral calls, worship, or lawful church governance.

Funding conditions apply to grant stewardship, not church office.
