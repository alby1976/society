# Staff Member 5-15 Report

## Template Instructions

This template is for staff members of the Society or an affiliated organization.

The report should help supervisors understand progress, workload, risks, support needed, and mission alignment. It is not intended to be a diary, a time sheet replacement, or a performance theatre. Keep it useful.

Confidential employment, pastoral, legal, housing, grant, or beneficiary details should be handled according to [[5-15 Report Confidentiality Policy]].
