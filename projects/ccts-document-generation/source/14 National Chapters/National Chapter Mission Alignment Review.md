# National Chapter Mission Alignment Review

## 1. Purpose

This document provides a chapter-specific Mission Alignment Review process for National Chapters of the Christian Community Transformation Society.

The review exists to ensure that each National Chapter remains faithful to the Society's doctrine, mission, governance standards, reserved powers, reporting duties, church-supporting identity, and local legal responsibilities.

This document applies the general [[Mission Alignment Review]] system to National Chapter life.

---

# 2. Governing Principle

A National Chapter must be locally embodied but internationally aligned.

A chapter may adapt to its country, language, law, and church context. It may not become a separate mission, a local personality project, a donor-controlled ministry, or a national franchise loosely attached to the Society's name.

---

# 3. Review Frequency

National Chapter Mission Alignment Review should occur:

- before full recognition;
- annually during provisional status;
- annually for the first three years after recognition;
- at least every three years once stable;
- after serious incidents;
- after leadership transition;
- after major ministry launch;
- after significant legal, financial, or church relationship concern;
- when the International Board requires review;
- when mission drift is suspected.

---

# 4. Review Authority

The review may be conducted by:

- International Board delegate;
- Mission Alignment Review team;
- National Chapter Board with external reviewer;
- governance committee;
- theological reviewer where doctrine is involved;
- church advisory reviewer where church relationships are involved;
- qualified external reviewer where required.

The reviewer should not have an unmanaged conflict of interest.

# 6. Review Questions

## 6.1 Doctrine and Mission

- Does the chapter affirm and preserve the Society's Statement of Faith?
- Does the chapter understand and support the Society's mission?
- Is the chapter protecting the Society/church distinction?
- Has local adaptation changed doctrine or mission in practice?
- Are leaders able to explain the Society's purpose clearly?
- Are ministries serving the mission rather than merely continuing because they exist?

## 6.2 Governance

- Is the National Chapter Board functioning?
- Are reserved powers respected?
- Are delegated authorities clear?
- Are decisions recorded?
- Are conflicts of interest disclosed and managed?
- Are required policies adopted and used?
- Is the chapter operating within recognition conditions?
- Are local bylaws or legal documents consistent with international standards?

## 6.3 Leadership

- Are leaders qualified?
- Are leaders reporting?
- Are leaders overloaded?
- Are leaders spending appropriate time with God and family where applicable?
- Are leaders accountable?
- Are succession needs identified?
- Are formation team leaders staying within authority?

## 6.4 Reporting and Institutional Memory

- Are required reports submitted on time?
- Are reports reviewed and acted upon?
- Are document and decision registers maintained?
- Are major lessons preserved?
- Are annual reports complete?
- Are future leaders able to understand what happened and why?

## 6.5 Church Relationships

- Are local church relationships healthy?
- Is the chapter receiving appropriate church advisory input?
- Is the chapter supporting rather than replacing churches?
- Are church referrals handled carefully?
- Are churches being respected rather than controlled?
- Are any church relationship concerns unresolved?

## 6.6 Financial Stewardship

- Are financial controls functioning?
- Are budgets approved?
- Are restricted funds tracked?
- Are expenses authorized?
- Are audits or reviews completed where required?
- Are donor restrictions mission-aligned?
- Are financial risks reported?

## 6.7 Ministry Oversight

- Are chapter ministries authorized and governed?
- Are chaplaincy, housing, cooperative, or foundation ministries operating within policy?
- Are ministries reporting?
- Are high-risk ministries reviewed?
- Are ministries being launched faster than governance capacity can support?

## 6.8 Legal and Local Adaptation

- Is the chapter compliant with local law?
- Are required filings current?
- Are local adaptations documented and approved?
- Are legal risks reviewed?
- Are translations accurate and controlled?
- Are local requirements being used appropriately rather than as an excuse for mission drift?

## 6.9 Risk

- Are risks tracked in [[National Chapter Risk Register]]?
- Are high risks escalated?
- Are incidents reported?
- Are safeguards, privacy, finance, and church concerns monitored?
- Are corrective actions completed?

---

# 7. Rating Scale

Each area may be rated as follows:

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Strong | Clearly aligned and healthy |
| Stable | Generally aligned with ordinary improvements needed |
| Watch | Concerns exist and require monitoring |
| Corrective Action Required | Significant concerns require action |
| Critical | Serious mission, legal, financial, doctrine, leadership, or governance concern |

---

# 8. Review Findings

The review should produce findings in these categories:

1. strengths;
2. concerns;
3. risks;
4. required corrections;
5. recommended improvements;
6. decisions needed;
7. policy revisions needed;
8. local adaptations needing review;
9. follow-up date;
10. responsible persons.

---

# 9. Corrective Action

Corrective actions may include:

- reporting improvement plan;
- leadership support or restructuring;
- legal review;
- financial control improvement;
- conflict of interest remedy;
- church advisory consultation;
- theological review;
- policy adoption;
- local adaptation correction;
- ministry pause;
- recognition condition update;
- restriction of authority;
- suspension review.

A chapter review without follow-up is institutional fog with headings.
