# Master Governance Dashboard

## 1. Purpose

This dashboard is the central navigation page for the Christian Community Transformation Society governance vault.

It is designed to reduce mental load by giving one clear place to review the governance system, find documents, track decisions, and prepare releases.

# 9. Review Questions

Use these questions during governance review:

- Are documents current?
- Are any documents missing?
- Are decisions recorded?
- Are reports being submitted?
- Are risks reviewed?
- Are leaders overloaded?
- Are local chapters aligned?
- Are ministries operating within authority?
- Are church boundaries clear?
- Are public communications accurate?
