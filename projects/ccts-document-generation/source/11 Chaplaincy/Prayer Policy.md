# Prayer Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the role of prayer in the Christian Community Transformation Society.

Prayer is not decorative. It is a foundational expression of dependence on God and should accompany the Society's founding, governance, ministry, reporting, care, expansion, correction, and thanksgiving.

This policy should be read with [[Chaplaincy Framework]], [[Chaplain Policy]], [[Spiritual Care Policy]], [[Annual Day of Prayer]], and [[Mission Statement]].

---

# 2. Governing Principle

The Society shall pursue its mission prayerfully.

Prayer should not be used to avoid planning, and planning should not be used to avoid prayer. The Society needs both dependence and diligence.

---

# 3. Prayer and Governance

Prayer should ordinarily be included in:

- board meetings;
- committee meetings;
- major governance decisions;
- national chapter formation;
- ministry approval;
- mission alignment review;
- executive appointment;
- chaplain appointment;
- housing decisions;
- cooperative launch;
- foundation grant strategy;
- crisis response;
- annual review.

Prayer reminds leaders that the Society is stewardship, not empire.

---

# 4. Prayer and Founding Roadmap

Prayer should accompany each major stage of the Society's founding and expansion.

This includes:

- vision refinement;
- governance formation;
- leadership development;
- worker cooperative pilot;
- incorporation;
- church partnerships;
- chaplaincy;
- housing;
- foundation development;
- international expansion;
- national chapter launch.

The Society should not move through stages as if prayer were merely a ribbon-cutting ceremony.

---

# 5. Prayer and Local Churches

The Society's prayer practices should support faithful local churches and not replace Lord's Day worship.

Prayer gatherings, devotionals, and annual prayer events must be clearly distinguished from church worship.

The Society may pray with and for churches, but it does not become a church.

---

# 6. Prayer Gatherings

The Society may hold prayer gatherings for:

- board members;
- staff;
- volunteers;
- residents;
- workers;
- national chapters;
- ministries;
- annual review;
- crisis response;
- thanksgiving;
- mission needs;
- international expansion.

Prayer gatherings should be voluntary unless attached to a defined leadership or governance duty.

---

# 7. Prayer Requests

Prayer requests may be gathered through:

- 5-15 reports;
- chaplain reports;
- ministry reports;
- board reports;
- church partners;
- national chapters;
- staff and volunteers;
- Annual Day of Prayer preparation.

Prayer requests should be handled with confidentiality and discretion.

---

# 8. Confidentiality in Prayer

Prayer requests must not become gossip.

A prayer request should not include sensitive personal, pastoral, medical, financial, housing, employment, legal, or family details unless the person has consented or there is a proper ministry reason.

Appropriate wording:

> Pray for wisdom in supporting a family facing serious stress.

Inappropriate wording:

> detailed private facts that are unnecessary for prayer.

---

# 9. Prayer and Chaplaincy

The chaplain may help gather, organize, and present prayer themes.

The chaplain may:

- pray with persons receiving spiritual care;
- prepare prayer guides;
- support Annual Day of Prayer;
- provide devotional meditation where authorized;
- identify prayer themes from reports without revealing confidential details.

---

# 10. Prayer and Reporting

Reports should include prayer requests where appropriate.

Prayer requests in reports should identify:

- thanksgiving;
- needs;
- wisdom required;
- mission concerns;
- people served;
- workers and volunteers;
- churches;
- national chapters;
- risks;
- upcoming decisions.

Confidentiality must be respected.

---

# 11. Prayer and Crisis

In crisis, the Society should pray and act.

Crisis prayer may be appropriate for:

- death or grief;
- serious illness;
- safeguarding concern;
- housing emergency;
- financial crisis;
- legal challenge;
- church relationship crisis;
- national chapter crisis;
- conflict;
- public controversy;
- major decision.

Prayer must not replace required reporting, safeguarding, legal compliance, or practical response.

---

# 12. Annual Day of Prayer

The Society shall maintain an [[Annual Day of Prayer]] framework.

The Annual Day of Prayer may include:

- thanksgiving;
- confession;
- supplication;
- intercession;
- mission renewal;
- prayer for churches;
- prayer for workers;
- prayer for residents;
- prayer for national chapters;
- prayer for vulnerable people;
- prayer for international expansion.

---

# 13. Prayer and International Expansion

Before entering a new country or recognizing a national chapter, the Society should commit the process to prayer.

Prayer should include:

- wisdom;
- church relationships;
- legal clarity;
- qualified leaders;
- financial stewardship;
- cultural humility;
- protection from mission drift;
- care for vulnerable people;
- faithful local implementation.

---

# 14. Prayer and Decision Records

Major decisions may include a brief note that prayer was offered.

This should not become performative. The point is not to prove spirituality in minutes, but to remember dependence.

---

# 15. Review

This policy should be reviewed annually with [[Annual Day of Prayer]], [[Chaplaincy Framework]], and [[Reporting Framework]].
