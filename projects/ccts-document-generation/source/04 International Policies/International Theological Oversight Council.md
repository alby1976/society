# International Theological Oversight Council

## Purpose

To provide theological review and doctrinal protection for the international Society.

## Adopted Principles

- The Council advises and reviews theology.
- The Board governs.
- The Council helps protect doctrinal identity across countries.
- The Council should include international theological counsel as the Society grows.

## Key Decisions

- The Council reviews doctrinal concerns, confessional standards, theological disputes, and mission alignment issues involving doctrine.

## International Application

This document is written for an international society. National chapters may localize procedures required by civil law, but they may not alter the Society's theology, mission, reserved powers, or core governance identity without approval under the international governance framework.
