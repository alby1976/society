# Membership Application and Admission Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the process for applying to and being admitted into membership in the Christian Community Transformation Society.

The purpose of admission is not merely to grow a list of names. The purpose is to receive persons into a defined organizational relationship that supports the Society's mission, doctrine, governance, and accountability.

---

# 2. Scope

This policy applies to applicants for Society membership, including membership in international or national chapter categories where applicable.

National chapters may implement local procedures consistent with this policy and local law.

---

# 3. Admission Principles

Membership admission should be:

- clear;
- fair;
- documented;
- proportionate to membership category;
- mission-aligned;
- respectful of privacy;
- consistent with doctrine;
- administratively simple enough to use;
- serious enough to matter.

The Society should avoid both gatekeeping theater and careless admission. One blocks people unnecessarily; the other leaves the windows open during a governance thunderstorm.

---

# 4. Application Requirements

An application may include:

1. full legal name;
2. preferred name;
3. contact information;
4. national chapter or country;
5. requested membership category;
6. local church information where appropriate;
7. statement of mission support;
8. doctrinal affirmation or acknowledgment required by category;
9. agreement to [[Membership Covenant]];
10. conflict of interest disclosure where relevant;
11. volunteer or ministry interest;
12. consent to data handling according to privacy policy;
13. signature or electronic acknowledgment;
14. date.

---

# 5. Doctrinal Requirements by Category

Doctrinal requirements should match the membership category.

Examples:

- Voting Members may be required to affirm the [[Statement of Faith]].
- Supporting Members may be required to respect the Society's doctrine and agree not to oppose it.
- Board Members must meet [[Board Member Qualifications]].
- Chaplains and theological teachers may require fuller confessional standards.
- Church Partners are reviewed under church partnership standards.
- Volunteer Participants may be required to respect the mission and conduct policies.

No applicant should be admitted into a category that gives influence over doctrine while rejecting the Society's doctrine.

---

# 6. Application Review

Applications should be reviewed by an authorized person or body.

The review should consider:

- completeness;
- eligibility;
- membership category;
- mission alignment;
- doctrinal requirements;
- conduct concerns;
- conflicts of interest;
- church relationship where appropriate;
- safeguarding or role-specific concerns;
- legal eligibility where relevant.

The review should be documented.

---

# 7. Interview or Conversation

Some membership categories may require an interview or orientation conversation.

The purpose may be to:

- explain the Society's mission;
- clarify that the Society is not a church;
- review membership responsibilities;
- answer questions;
- confirm membership category;
- identify volunteer or service interests;
- discuss concerns.

An interview should be pastoral in tone but organizational in function.

---

# 8. Approval Authority

Approval authority should follow bylaws, chapter policy, and the [[Authority Matrix]].

Possible approving authorities include:

- membership coordinator;
- National Executive Director;
- National Board;
- International Executive Director;
- International Board;
- membership committee.

Voting membership, board membership, and roles involving authority should require more formal approval than supporting membership.

---

# 9. Admission Decision

An application may be:

- approved;
- approved for a different category;
- deferred pending more information;
- conditionally approved;
- denied;
- referred to another process.

The applicant should be informed of the result.

---

# 10. Conditional Admission

Conditional admission may be appropriate where:

- orientation is pending;
- conflict disclosure needs review;
- local church information is incomplete;
- training is required;
- safeguarding screening is pending;
- membership category needs clarification.

Conditional members may have limited rights until conditions are satisfied.

---

# 11. Denial of Application

An application may be denied for reasons such as:

- failure to meet eligibility requirements;
- refusal to affirm or respect required doctrine;
- serious mission misalignment;
- unresolved misconduct concerns;
- false or misleading information;
- unmanaged conflict of interest;
- safeguarding concern;
- legal concern;
- disruptive or harmful conduct;
- attempted membership for improper influence.

Denial should be documented.

Where appropriate, the applicant may be invited to reapply later.

---

# 12. Appeal or Review of Denial

An applicant denied membership may request review if policy allows.

Review should consider whether:

- the process was fair;
- facts were correct;
- criteria were applied properly;
- conflicts of interest affected the decision;
- the applicant has provided new information.

Appeal rights may vary by membership category and law.

---

# 13. Membership Orientation

New members should receive orientation.

Orientation may include:

- [[Statement of Faith]];
- [[Mission Statement]];
- [[Vision Statement]];
- [[Core Values]];
- [[Membership Covenant]];
- church-supporting boundaries;
- reporting expectations;
- confidentiality;
- conflict of interest;
- volunteer pathways;
- national chapter structure;
- member rights and responsibilities.

Orientation may be written, video, in-person, or online.

---

# 14. Membership Registry Entry

Upon admission, the member should be entered into the membership registry.

The registry should record:

- name;
- membership category;
- national chapter;
- admission date;
- approval authority;
- status;
- review or renewal date;
- voting eligibility if applicable;
- signed acknowledgments;
- relevant notes.

---

# 15. Privacy

Membership information shall be handled according to privacy policy.

The Society should collect only information needed for membership, governance, safeguarding, reporting, or legal requirements.

---

# 16. Renewal

Some membership categories may require periodic renewal.

Renewal may include:

- updated contact information;
- reaffirmation of mission;
- updated conflict disclosure;
- continued eligibility review;
- church information update;
- updated covenant acknowledgment.

---

# 17. International and National Coordination

Where membership is held through a national chapter, the national chapter shall maintain records and report summary membership data to the International Society according to policy.

The International Society may require direct records for voting members, board members, international members, or persons holding cross-border authority.

---
