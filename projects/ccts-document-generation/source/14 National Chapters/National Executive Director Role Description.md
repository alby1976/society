# National Executive Director Role Description

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the role of the National Executive Director or equivalent senior executive leader of a National Chapter.

The National Executive Director leads day-to-day national chapter operations under the authority of the National Chapter Board and within the delegated authority granted by the International Society.

---

# 2. Governing Principle

The National Executive Director is a servant-leader and steward, not a founder-king.

The role requires operational leadership, reporting discipline, mission clarity, legal awareness, church-supporting posture, and willingness to be accountable.

---

# 3. Appointment

The National Executive Director should be appointed by the National Chapter Board according to chapter bylaws, local law, and international governance requirements.

International approval may be required where reserved powers apply.

The appointment should be documented.

---

# 4. Core Responsibilities

The National Executive Director is responsible to:

1. implement board decisions;
2. lead national operations;
3. supervise staff and ministry leaders;
4. ensure reporting;
5. coordinate national ministries;
6. manage budget implementation;
7. identify risks;
8. maintain legal and operational compliance;
9. preserve mission alignment;
10. support church relationships;
11. coordinate with International Society leadership;
12. maintain institutional memory;
13. oversee document and decision records;
14. prepare reports for the Board;
15. escalate serious concerns.

---

# 5. Authority

Authority should be defined in writing.

The National Executive Director may have authority to:

- manage staff;
- approve routine expenses within limits;
- sign routine contracts within limits;
- coordinate ministries;
- prepare budgets;
- communicate with partners;
- submit reports;
- convene operational meetings;
- implement policies;
- make urgent operational decisions within delegated authority.

The role may not exceed delegated authority or reserved powers.

---

# 6. Limits of Authority

The National Executive Director may not independently:

- change doctrine;
- change mission;
- override the Board;
- approve major restricted gifts beyond authority;
- acquire or dispose of major assets;
- launch major ministries requiring approval;
- alter reserved powers;
- bind the chapter to major liabilities without approval;
- conceal risks from the Board;
- control local churches;
- replace local church leadership.

---

# 7. Reporting Duties

The National Executive Director shall submit regular reports using [[National Executive Director 5-15 Report]] or another approved reporting format.

Reports should include:

- chapter health;
- ministry updates;
- finances;
- risks;
- church relationships;
- legal and compliance matters;
- staffing;
- decisions needed;
- prayer requests;
- mission alignment concerns.

---

# 8. Relationship to National Board

The National Executive Director reports to the National Chapter Board.

The Board should provide:

- clear authority;
- supervision;
- performance review;
- support;
- correction;
- strategic direction;
- accountability;
- protection from role overload.

---

# 9. Relationship to International Society

The National Executive Director may communicate with the International Society as authorized.

Responsibilities may include:

- submitting international reports;
- participating in Mission Alignment Review;
- communicating chapter developments;
- escalating risks;
- requesting approvals;
- implementing international policy;
- maintaining document alignment.

---

# 10. Relationship to Churches

The National Executive Director should cultivate appropriate church-supporting relationships.

The role should respect that the Society is not a church and must not control local churches.

The Executive Director may coordinate with church advisors, church partners, and chaplains as appropriate.

---

# 11. Ministry Oversight

The Executive Director may oversee national ministries such as:

- chaplaincy;
- housing;
- worker cooperative;
- Church Support Foundation;
- prayer gatherings;
- reporting systems;
- leadership development;
- church partnerships.

Each ministry must operate under approved policy and authority.

---

# 12. Financial Responsibilities

The Executive Director may be responsible for:

- budget preparation;
- expense oversight;
- financial reporting;
- restricted fund tracking support;
- grant compliance;
- financial risk escalation;
- working with treasurer or finance officer.

The Executive Director should not be the only person controlling money.

---

# 13. Risk Responsibilities

The Executive Director should ensure risks are identified and escalated.

Risks may involve legal compliance, finances, staff, volunteers, ministries, church relationships, safeguarding, privacy, public reputation, and mission drift.

---

# 14. Qualifications

The National Executive Director should demonstrate:

- commitment to the Society's mission;
- governance understanding;
- operational competence;
- financial awareness;
- reporting discipline;
- church-supporting posture;
- communication ability;
- humility;
- resilience;
- confidentiality;
- willingness to be corrected.

---

# 15. Workload and Spiritual Health

The Executive Director's workload should be reviewed.

Leadership qualification includes attention to time with God and family where applicable.

If workload prevents healthy spiritual and family responsibilities, the Board should consider reducing workload, adding support, restructuring duties, or requiring leave.

If the problem does not improve, removal or suspension from leadership may be appropriate until circumstances change.

This is not punishment. It is protection for the person, their household, and the Society.

---

# 16. Performance Review

The Board should review the Executive Director at least annually.

Review should consider:

- mission alignment;
- reporting;
- staff leadership;
- financial stewardship;
- church relationships;
- risk management;
- board communication;
- institutional memory;
- workload health;
- compliance with authority.

---

# 17. Removal or Suspension

The Executive Director may be removed or suspended for:

- misconduct;
- mission drift;
- doctrinal misalignment;
- refusal of accountability;
- financial concern;
- failure to report;
- serious conflict of interest;
- breach of confidentiality;
- legal noncompliance;
- unsafe leadership;
- inability to fulfill duties.

The process must follow local law, bylaws, and policy.

---

# 18. Review

This role description should be reviewed annually during chapter launch and whenever the role is substantially changed.
