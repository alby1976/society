# Reporting Framework

## Executive Summary

The Society's reporting system preserves accountability, reduces memory loss, and supports governance review. Reporting should be lightweight enough for regular use but detailed enough for stewardship and supporter communication.

## Adopted Principles

- Weekly and monthly reporting should be simple and sustainable.
- Annual reporting should be more detailed and suitable for supporter updates.
- Reports should feed the Mission Alignment Review system.
- Reporting should preserve institutional memory.

## Reporting Levels

### Weekly or Monthly 5-15 Report

A brief report taking approximately 15 minutes to write and 5 minutes to read.

### Monthly Ministry Report

Expanded report covering activities, needs, risks, finances, prayer items, and decisions.

### Annual Ministry Report

Comprehensive report suitable for board review, supporter communication, and long-term archival use.

## Key Decisions

- 5-15 reporting may occur weekly to monthly.
- Annual reports provide more detail and may be used for periodic reporting to supporters.
