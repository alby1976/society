# Hong Kong Chapter Local Adaptation Register

## 1. Purpose

This register tracks Hong Kong-specific adaptations to international Society governance documents, policies, templates, procedures, language, and public communications.

The register exists to preserve international alignment while allowing lawful and wise Hong Kong implementation.

---

# 2. Governing Principle

Local adaptation must be visible, reviewable, and bounded.

Unrecorded adaptation becomes future confusion. Recorded adaptation becomes governance memory.

# 4. Adaptation Categories

Use one or more categories:

- legal structure;
- governance terminology;
- language;
- Traditional Chinese translation;
- Cantonese communication;
- English public communication;
- finance;
- fundraising;
- privacy;
- employment;
- volunteer management;
- safeguarding;
- housing;
- worker cooperative;
- Foundation grants;
- church relationships;
- public communications;
- local calendar;
- other.

---

# 5. Required Review Fields

Each adaptation should record:

- international source document;
- Hong Kong-specific change;
- reason for change;
- whether legal review was obtained;
- whether International Board approval is required;
- whether mission alignment is affected;
- effective date;
- review date;
- responsible person.

---

# 6. Adaptations Requiring International Approval

Hong Kong Chapter adaptations require International Board or delegated international approval if they affect:

- doctrine;
- mission;
- reserved powers;
- governance authority;
- chapter recognition;
- public identity;
- church recognition;
- chaplain qualifications;
- grant eligibility;
- housing eligibility;
- cooperative structure;
- financial controls;
- reporting requirements;
- conflict of interest;
- safeguarding;
- privacy at a high-risk level.

---

# 7. Minor Local Adaptations

Minor local adaptations may be recorded without formal international approval if they do not affect core identity, authority, risk, or policy meaning.

Examples:

- local address;
- local contact person;
- local date format;
- local administrative title;
- local meeting schedule;
- local form labels;
- ordinary language adjustments that do not change meaning.

---

# 8. Legal Adaptations

Legal adaptations should include a legal note or reference to legal review.

Legal adaptations may involve:

- legal form;
- registration;
- officer or board requirements;
- banking;
- fundraising;
- employment;
- privacy;
- housing;
- cooperative or enterprise structure;
- grant-making;
- cross-border transfers.

---

# 9. Language and Translation Adaptations

Hong Kong documents may require English and Traditional Chinese versions.

Translation records should include:

- source document title;
- source document version;
- target language;
- translator;
- reviewer;
- theological review if needed;
- legal review if needed;
- approval date;
- review date.

Translation should preserve meaning and should not quietly become a new policy.

---

# 10. Public Communications

Public communications should be adapted carefully.

The register should track changes to:

- public name;
- chapter status description;
- donation language;
- ministry descriptions;
- church relationship descriptions;
- website text;
- social media descriptions;
- grant announcements;
- recruitment materials.

No public communication should imply recognition or authority not yet granted.

---

# 11. Review Schedule

The register should be reviewed:

- quarterly during exploration;
- before formation team authorization;
- before provisional recognition;
- before public launch;
- annually after recognition;
- whenever law, language, governance, or ministry structure changes.

---

# 12. Retired Adaptations

When an adaptation is no longer needed, it should be marked retired rather than deleted.

The retirement record should include:

- reason;
- date;
- approving authority;
- replacement document or policy if any.

---

# 13. Review

This register should be reviewed quarterly during Hong Kong formation and at least annually after recognition.
