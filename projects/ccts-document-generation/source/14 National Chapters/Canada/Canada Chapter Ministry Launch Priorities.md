# Canada Chapter Ministry Launch Priorities

## 1. Purpose

This document establishes a recommended ministry launch order for the Canada Chapter.

The purpose is to prevent the chapter from attempting every ministry at once and to ensure that prayer, governance, reporting, leadership, legal readiness, and risk controls are in place before complex ministries begin.

---

# 2. Governing Principle

Ministry should launch in the order of readiness, not excitement.

A ministry that requires more legal, financial, or governance capacity should not launch before the chapter can carry that responsibility.

---

# 3. Recommended Launch Sequence

The recommended Canada Chapter launch sequence is:

1. prayer and discernment;
2. governance formation;
3. reporting and institutional memory setup;
4. church advisory relationships;
5. leadership development;
6. worker cooperative pilot;
7. chaplaincy implementation;
8. housing feasibility review;
9. Church Support Foundation readiness;
10. Foundation grant activity;
11. housing implementation if ready;
12. annual review and expansion.

---

# 4. Stage 1: Prayer and Discernment

The Canada Chapter should begin with sustained prayer.

Deliverables:

- prayer record;
- formation reflection;
- initial mission fit statement;
- leadership discernment notes;
- church relationship prayer list.

Prayer is not a decorative launch step. It is part of governance dependence.

---

# 5. Stage 2: Governance Formation

Before ministries launch, the chapter should establish:

- formation team or provisional board;
- conflict of interest process;
- decision register;
- document registry;
- reporting calendar;
- authority matrix;
- legal readiness checklist;
- risk register.

---

# 6. Stage 3: Reporting and Institutional Memory

The chapter should implement reporting before major operations begin.

Deliverables:

- National Executive Director 5-15 Report process if role exists;
- National Board Summary process;
- ministry report templates;
- document registry;
- decision register;
- annual chapter report template;
- records storage plan.

If the chapter cannot remember decisions, it should not multiply them.

---

# 7. Stage 4: Church Advisory Relationships

The chapter should seek church advisory relationships before launching spiritual care or church-connected ministries.

Deliverables:

- list of potential advisors;
- church relationship principles;
- referral pathway draft;
- seminary recognition pathway;
- chaplaincy advice plan;
- prayer partner churches where appropriate.

---

# 8. Stage 5: Leadership Development

The chapter should prepare leaders before assigning heavy responsibilities.

Deliverables:

- leadership qualifications review;
- workload review;
- role descriptions;
- board orientation;
- executive role clarity;
- ministry leader training;
- reporting training.

Leadership health includes time with God and family where applicable.

---

# 9. Stage 6: Worker Cooperative Pilot

The worker cooperative pilot is recommended before Foundation launch because it can be smaller, more concrete, and more operationally testable.

Before launch, complete:

- pilot cooperative policy review;
- legal and employment review;
- budget;
- manager appointment;
- worker status clarity;
- customer and contract policy;
- safety and risk policy;
- reporting plan;
- Mission Alignment Review criteria.

The pilot should be time-limited and evaluated before scaling.

---

# 10. Stage 7: Chaplaincy Implementation

Chaplaincy should be implemented once church advisory and theological standards are ready.

Before launch, complete:

- chaplaincy framework;
- chaplain policy;
- chaplain qualifications;
- seminary recognition policy;
- spiritual care policy;
- confidentiality rules;
- chaplain report process;
- church referral policy.

Chaplaincy should support, not replace, local churches.

---

# 11. Stage 8: Housing Feasibility Review

Housing should begin with feasibility review, not property acquisition.

Before any property commitment, complete:

- housing trust framework review;
- housing legal review;
- affordability model;
- property acquisition policy;
- tenant and resident policy;
- lease standards;
- maintenance reserve plan;
- risk register;
- financial model;
- Mission Alignment Review.

Housing is heavy. Do not pick it up casually.

---

# 12. Stage 9: Church Support Foundation Readiness

Foundation work should begin after governance, finance, conflict, donor, grant, and reporting systems are ready.

Before grant-making, complete:

- Foundation framework;
- grant policy;
- grant application process;
- restricted gift policy;
- grant agreement template;
- grant reporting policy;
- conflict process;
- financial controls;
- donor communication rules.

---

# 13. Stage 10: Grant Activity

Grant activity may begin when Foundation readiness is confirmed.

Start with:

- small grants;
- clear categories;
- simple reporting;
- limited restricted funds;
- board review;
- annual review.

Avoid complex grants before systems are proven.

---

# 14. Stage 11: Housing Implementation

Housing implementation should occur only after legal, financial, governance, and operational readiness.

Implementation may begin with:

- partnerships;
- housing support referral;
- feasibility study;
- small pilot;
- leased arrangement;
- property due diligence;
- community land trust exploration.

Do not acquire property before governance can carry it.

---

# 15. Stage 12: Annual Review and Expansion

Each year, the Canada Chapter should review:

- mission alignment;
- legal status;
- finances;
- leadership health;
- ministry outcomes;
- reporting discipline;
- church relationships;
- risk;
- readiness to expand.

Expansion should follow demonstrated faithfulness, not pressure.

---

# 16. Launch Gate Checklist

| Gate | Required Before Moving Forward | Complete? |
|---|---|---|
| Governance Gate | Board, authority, conflict policy, reporting | Yes / No |
| Church Gate | Church advisory relationships and referral clarity | Yes / No |
| Legal Gate | Legal review for ministry type | Yes / No |
| Finance Gate | Budget, controls, reporting, reserves where needed | Yes / No |
| Risk Gate | Risk register and escalation process | Yes / No |
| Mission Gate | Mission Alignment Review complete | Yes / No |
| Prayer Gate | Prayer and discernment recorded | Yes / No |

---

# 17. Review

This document should be reviewed annually during Canada Chapter formation and whenever ministry launch order changes.
