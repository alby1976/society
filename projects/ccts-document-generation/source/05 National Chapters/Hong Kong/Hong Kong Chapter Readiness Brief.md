# Hong Kong Chapter Readiness Brief

## Purpose

To prepare for possible future expansion into Hong Kong without prematurely assuming legal feasibility or ministry readiness.

## Adopted Principles

- Hong Kong should not copy Canadian law.
- The chapter must inherit international theology and mission.
- Legal, church, financial, and cross-cultural readiness must be assessed before launch.
- Translation and localization are allowed; theological rewriting is not.

## Key Decisions

- Hong Kong should begin as a readiness project, not an automatic chapter.
- A legal localization guide, church advisory contacts, and launch checklist are needed before formal recognition.

## International Application

This document is written for an international society. National chapters may localize procedures required by civil law, but they may not alter the Society's theology, mission, reserved powers, or core governance identity without approval under the international governance framework.
