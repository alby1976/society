# Society Boundaries

## Executive Summary

This policy defines what the Society is and is not. These boundaries protect the Society from mission drift and protect local churches from improper institutional overreach.

## The Society May

- Support faithful local churches.
- Create ministries that serve Christian discipleship, mercy, work, housing, and community transformation.
- Own or oversee supporting organizations.
- Coordinate shared ministry efforts.
- Provide chaplaincy, training, grants, reporting, and institutional support.

## The Society Shall Not

- Replace the local church.
- Administer sacraments.
- Exercise church discipline.
- Replace elders, pastors, or church courts.
- Treat Society events as substitutes for Lord's Day worship.

## Key Decisions

- Church authority remains with local churches.
- Society ministry must point people toward faithful churches.
- The Society's chaplain must not contradict local church leadership.
