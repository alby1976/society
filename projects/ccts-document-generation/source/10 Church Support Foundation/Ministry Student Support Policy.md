# Ministry Student Support Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs Foundation support for ministry students, theological students, ministry trainees, and persons preparing for church-connected service.

The policy exists to support faithful preparation for ministry while protecting doctrine, stewardship, accountability, and pastoral sensitivity.

---

# 2. Governing Principle

The Foundation may support ministry preparation, but it must not pretend to control God's calling or the church's office-bearing decisions.

Funding can support preparation. It cannot manufacture vocation.

---

# 3. Eligible Students

Eligible students may include:

- theological students;
- ministry trainees;
- seminary students;
- chaplaincy trainees;
- leadership development participants;
- language or translation ministry trainees;
- persons preparing for church-connected service;
- persons approved by recognized church or ministry bodies.

Eligibility must be defined by grant category.

---

# 4. Seminary Recognition

Support for formal theological education should ordinarily be limited to institutions recognized under [[Seminary Recognition Policy]] or approved by the appropriate theological or church advisory authority.

Exceptions should be documented.

---

# 5. Application Requirements

Applications may request:

- name and contact information;
- church membership or church connection;
- program of study;
- institution;
- ministry goal;
- budget;
- amount requested;
- other funding;
- references;
- academic status;
- doctrinal affirmation where appropriate;
- conflict of interest disclosure;
- reporting agreement.

Only necessary information should be collected.

---

# 6. Review Criteria

Applications should be reviewed according to:

- doctrinal alignment;
- church connection;
- recognized training path;
- ministry purpose;
- financial need;
- academic readiness;
- character references;
- accountability;
- mission fit;
- risk;
- ability to report.

---

# 7. Approved Uses

Support may be used for:

- tuition;
- required books;
- required fees;
- approved training costs;
- approved travel;
- language study;
- ministry internship expenses;
- examination fees;
- required technology;
- other approved education-related costs.

---

# 8. Prohibited Uses

Support may not be used for:

- expenses outside the approved grant;
- private benefit unrelated to study;
- luxury expenses;
- unapproved debt repayment;
- activities contrary to doctrine;
- political partisan activity;
- unsupported cash transfers;
- unauthorized program changes.

---

# 9. Grant Agreement

A student support grant should use a written agreement.

The agreement should define:

- amount;
- permitted uses;
- institution or program;
- reporting;
- transcript or confirmation requirements where appropriate;
- change of program process;
- withdrawal process;
- repayment conditions if any;
- final report;
- confidentiality.

---

# 10. Reporting

Students may be required to report:

- enrollment status;
- course completion;
- ministry training progress;
- use of funds;
- changes in program;
- prayer requests;
- church or mentor confirmation where appropriate;
- final outcome.

Reporting should be proportionate and not intrusive.

---

# 11. Change of Direction

A student may later not pursue the ministry path originally expected.

The Foundation's recommended response is:

1. do not assume bad faith;
2. ask for a final report;
3. confirm whether funds were used for approved purposes;
4. review whether the grant agreement included repayment conditions;
5. consider pastoral, academic, health, family, church, or providential reasons;
6. require repayment only where misuse occurred or written conditions require it;
7. document the outcome;
8. update future grant design if needed.

The Foundation should avoid punishing honest vocational discernment.

---

# 12. Withdrawal or Failure to Complete

If a student withdraws, fails, or does not complete the program, the Foundation should review:

- reason;
- timing;
- funds already used;
- refund available;
- reporting;
- whether misuse occurred;
- whether future support is appropriate;
- whether pastoral or church support is needed.

The response should be just, documented, and compassionate.

---

# 13. Church Relationship

Where appropriate, student support should involve or respect the student's local church.

The Foundation should not replace church discernment of gifts, calling, or office.

Church references or confirmations may be requested where appropriate.

---

# 14. Confidentiality

Student records should be protected.

Sensitive academic, financial, church, family, immigration, health, or pastoral information should be limited to authorized persons.

---

# 15. Conflicts of Interest

Conflicts may include:

- student related to decision-maker;
- student from decision-maker's church;
- donor-designated student;
- seminary relationship;
- staff or board involvement;
- family connection.

Conflicts must be disclosed and managed.

---

# 16. Review

This policy should be reviewed annually during Foundation launch and at least every three years once student support operations stabilize.
