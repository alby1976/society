# Stewardship Advisors

## Executive Summary

Stewardship Advisors preserve institutional memory, mentor current leaders, and provide historical continuity without holding governing authority.

## Adopted Principles

- Former board members and founders may serve as advisors.
- Advisors preserve memory without controlling future boards.
- Advisors help explain why past decisions were made.
- Advisors support succession and mission continuity.

## Key Decisions

- Founder advisory role may include past board members.
- Stewardship Advisors advise only and do not govern.
- Advisors should be connected to the Decision Register and governance archive.
