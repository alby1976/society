# Mission Theology

## Executive Summary

The Society's theology of mission is that the Gospel proclaimed and received in the church bears fruit in all of life. The Society exists to help believers live faithfully throughout the week through mercy, work, housing, discipleship, prayer, and stewardship.

## Adopted Principles

- The Society's work flows from the ordinary ministry of the Word in faithful churches.
- Community transformation begins with the Gospel, not institutional activism.
- Practical service must remain connected to Christian discipleship and worship.
- Ministries should strengthen the church's witness without confusing Society activity with church authority.

## Key Decisions

- The Society's purpose is not to replace Lord's Day corporate worship.
- The Society exists so that Lord's Day worship transforms Monday through Saturday.
- Ministries should point participants toward the local church.
