# Relationship with Local Churches

## Purpose

To define how the Society relates to local churches across all jurisdictions.

## Adopted Principles

- The Society strengthens local churches.
- The Society does not administer sacraments, replace elders, exercise church discipline, or conduct Lord’s Day worship as a church.
- Ministry should encourage participation in faithful local churches.

## Key Decisions

- Church Advisory Fellowship is advisory, not governing.
- Chaplains must respect local church leadership.
- Unchurched persons should be lovingly connected to faithful churches.

## International Application

This document is written for an international society. National chapters may localize procedures required by civil law, but they may not alter the Society's theology, mission, reserved powers, or core governance identity without approval under the international governance framework.
