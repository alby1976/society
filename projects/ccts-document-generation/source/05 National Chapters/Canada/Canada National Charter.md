# Canada National Charter

## Purpose

To define Canada as the founding national chapter of the international Society.

## Adopted Principles

- Canada is the founding chapter, not the theological or permanent global center.
- The Canadian chapter must implement international doctrine, mission, governance, and reserved powers.
- Canadian law shapes local bylaws, charity compliance, and operations.

## Key Decisions

- Canada should establish the initial legal platform.
- Canadian documents must remain subordinate to the International Constitution.
- Canadian ministry may pilot cooperative, housing, chaplaincy, and foundation models.

## International Application

This document is written for an international society. National chapters may localize procedures required by civil law, but they may not alter the Society's theology, mission, reserved powers, or core governance identity without approval under the international governance framework.
