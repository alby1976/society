# Annual Day of Prayer

## 1. Purpose

The Annual Day of Prayer is a Society-wide day set aside for prayer, thanksgiving, confession, intercession, supplication, mission renewal, and dependence on God.

It is intended to gather the Society's leaders, members, workers, volunteers, national chapters, ministries, and church partners around prayer for the Society's faithfulness and mission.

The Annual Day of Prayer is not a replacement for Lord's Day worship or the ordinary worship of the local church.

---

# 2. Governing Principle

The Society's work must be sustained by prayer.

The Annual Day of Prayer helps the Society remember that governance, housing, work, grants, chaplaincy, reporting, international expansion, and institutional memory are not merely organizational tasks. They are acts of stewardship under God.

---

# 3. Frequency

The Society should hold an Annual Day of Prayer once each year.

National chapters may hold local expressions of the Annual Day of Prayer, provided they remain aligned with the international framework.

Additional days of prayer may be called during crisis, expansion, or major decision seasons.

---

# 4. Leadership

The chaplain may facilitate the Annual Day of Prayer if authorized.

The chaplain may:

- coordinate prayer themes;
- prepare a prayer guide;
- gather prayer requests;
- lead portions of prayer;
- provide a devotional meditation;
- invite appropriate leaders to participate;
- coordinate with church advisors;
- help protect confidentiality.

The chaplain's role remains Society chaplaincy, not church office.

---

# 5. Devotional Meditation

The chaplain may provide a devotional meditation during the Annual Day of Prayer.

The meditation must:

- be consistent with [[Statement of Faith]];
- support the Society's mission;
- avoid replacing Lord's Day preaching;
- avoid contradicting local church leadership;
- be appropriate to the Society's role;
- be reviewable where needed.

The meditation should direct attention to God, not the institution.

---

# 6. Prayer Themes

Prayer themes may include:

- adoration of God;
- confession of sin and weakness;
- thanksgiving for mercy;
- dependence on Christ;
- guidance by the Holy Spirit;
- faithfulness to Scripture;
- protection from mission drift;
- local churches;
- church partners;
- national chapters;
- board and leaders;
- staff and volunteers;
- housing residents;
- workers and cooperatives;
- grant recipients;
- chaplaincy;
- vulnerable people;
- finances and stewardship;
- international expansion;
- future leaders;
- institutional memory;
- unity and humility.

---

# 7. ACTS Structure

The Society may use the ACTS structure:

## Adoration

Praising God for who He is.

## Confession

Confessing sin, weakness, pride, self-reliance, fear, and mission drift.

## Thanksgiving

Giving thanks for God's provision, mercy, churches, workers, donors, volunteers, residents, beneficiaries, and answered prayers.

## Supplication

Asking God for wisdom, faithfulness, provision, protection, repentance, unity, courage, and fruitfulness.

---

# 8. Gathering Format

The Annual Day of Prayer may include:

- in-person gathering;
- online gathering;
- national chapter gatherings;
- written prayer guide;
- regional prayer meetings;
- board prayer session;
- staff and volunteer prayer;
- church partner prayer;
- private prayer prompts.

The format may vary by country and capacity.

---

# 9. Relationship to Churches

The Annual Day of Prayer should invite cooperation with faithful local churches where appropriate.

Church partners may be invited to pray for the Society.

The Society should not schedule or present the day in a way that competes with Lord's Day worship.

---

# 10. Prayer Requests

Prayer requests may be gathered from:

- 5-15 reports;
- annual reports;
- board reports;
- national chapter reports;
- chaplain reports;
- church partners;
- staff and volunteers;
- ministries;
- mission alignment reviews.

Sensitive information must be summarized carefully.

---

# 11. Confidentiality

Prayer guides and public prayer should not include unnecessary confidential details.

Sensitive prayer requests should be generalized or restricted to appropriate leaders.

Confidentiality protects people and honors trust.

---

# 12. Annual Report Connection

The Annual Day of Prayer may be connected to annual reporting.

The Society may use the day to:

- give thanks for the prior year;
- pray over annual report themes;
- confess failures;
- seek wisdom for the coming year;
- pray for major decisions;
- remember founding principles.

---

# 13. National Chapters

Each national chapter may adapt the Annual Day of Prayer to local language, culture, time zone, and church context.

Local adaptation must preserve:

- biblical prayer;
- Society mission;
- church-supporting identity;
- confidentiality;
- international unity.

---

# 14. Documentation

The Society may preserve a non-confidential summary of the Annual Day of Prayer.

The summary may include:

- date;
- participants or chapters represented;
- prayer themes;
- Scripture used;
- thanksgiving items;
- major mission concerns;
- follow-up items.

Sensitive prayer details should not be archived publicly.
