# Annual National Chapter Report

## Template Instructions

This template is used to prepare an annual report for a National Chapter of the Christian Community Transformation Society.

The report should summarize chapter governance, ministries, church relationships, finances, risks, local adaptations, Mission Alignment Review, major decisions, lessons learned, and prayer needs.

The report should be preserved as part of institutional memory.
