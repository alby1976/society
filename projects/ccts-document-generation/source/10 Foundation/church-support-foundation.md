# Church Support Foundation

## Executive Summary

The Church Support Foundation is envisioned as a funding and support structure that provides grants, capacity-building support, and ministry development resources in alignment with the Society's mission.

## Adopted Principles

- Funding should strengthen faithful ministry rather than create dependence.
- Capacity-building grants are preferred where they increase long-term fruitfulness.
- Student or leadership funding should be linked to ministry commitment where appropriate.
- The Foundation should launch after the Society and pilot cooperative are more mature.

## Key Decisions

- The Foundation should not be the first major initiative because it requires more resources.
- Capacity-building grants are a core grant category.
- Grant policy should address what happens if a funded student does not pursue ministry.
