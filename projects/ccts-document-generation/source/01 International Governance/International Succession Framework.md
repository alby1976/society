# International Succession Framework

## Purpose

To preserve mission, theology, institutional memory, and leadership continuity across generations and countries.

## Adopted Principles

- The Society must not depend permanently on founding personalities.
- Former leaders may serve as advisors but not shadow governors.
- Succession must preserve mission without freezing wise adaptation.

## Key Decisions

- Stewardship Advisors preserve institutional memory.
- Leadership development precedes ministry expansion.
- International succession planning applies to the board, chapters, chaplaincy, and ministry entities.

## International Application

This document is written for an international society. National chapters may localize procedures required by civil law, but they may not alter the Society's theology, mission, reserved powers, or core governance identity without approval under the international governance framework.
